# DirectorLink v0.13.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

Scenes: one tap sets the whole house.

## Scenes

- A new **Scenes** tab. A scene sets several lights, the AC and the blinds with one tap, and can open the gate — for example *Good night*: all lights off, the bedroom AC to 24°, the blinds closed.
- **Easy to make** (admins): start from an idea (All off, Good night, Good morning, Leaving home, Cool the house) or a new scene, then **Add an action**: where (a room or the whole home), what (lights, AC, blinds, doors and gates) and what to do.
- **Just one light of six?** Tap **Choose** and pick the ones you want; the others stay as they are.
- **Copy the house as it is now:** set the lights, AC and blinds by hand, and the scene remembers them like that.
- **Try it now** before saving, and **Show on Home** puts a Run button for the scene at the top of Home.
- Everyone with member access runs scenes; viewers see them. A door or gate in a scene gets a short press, like its Open button, and never stays held; it runs only for keys with door access and while Door Control is on — otherwise it is skipped and the rest of the scene still runs. A scene that opens doors asks for a second tap.
- "All lights in the living room" includes lights added to the room later.

## Also

- Scenes are kept on your controller, survive driver updates, and work at home and away (through the end-to-end lock).
- API: `/v1/scenes` (list, create, change, delete), `POST /v1/scenes/{id}/run`, `POST /v1/scenes/try` — see `docs/SCENES.md`.

## Installing

Update DirectorLink in Composer (it loads without a reboot; keys, profiles and settings stay). The app updates by itself.

## Known issues

- Scenes do not run on a schedule yet: schedules by time and weather come next (0.14.0).
- An invitation has to be accepted with the email it was made for; letting the owner approve another address comes later.
- Only relay 1 of each KNX Contact/Relay device is controlled; KNX percentage dimming remains deferred (issue #11).
