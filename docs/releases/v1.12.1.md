# DirectorLink v1.12.1

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

The weather on Home, and the cameras in the order you choose.

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4 or Snap One.

## The weather on Home

Home now shows the weather in one line, right under the command field: *Now 27°  Today 23°–30°  rain 14%  sunset 18:13*. From 15:00 it gives tomorrow's low and high instead, so together they cover the next day. The chance of rain shows only from 10%, and only the next sunrise or sunset appears; an icon shows the sky, with a moon at night. It is the forecast your controller already keeps for schedules: the phone asks only the controller, at home or through your account, and nothing else on the internet. Whole degrees, in your project's °C or °F, for every user. Without a location in Composer there is no line (ADR-084).

## Cameras in your order

Admins can now set the order of the cameras for the whole home: on the Cameras tab, tap **Order**, then drag a camera by its handle or use the arrows (or the keyboard), as for rooms in Settings → Rooms, and Save. Everyone sees the cameras in that order: on the Cameras tab, where the first one is shown large, and inside each room, where doorbells follow their cameras. New cameras appear at the end until they are placed. The order is kept on the controller, survives updates and restarts, and is in backups.

## Installing

- **Update DirectorLink in Composer** as usual. The app updates by itself. The cloud is unchanged; no D1 migration.
- **Going back to 1.12.0:** nothing is lost. The camera order stays on the controller unread; a backup made by 1.12.1 is refused by 1.12.0, as for any newer backup.

## For developers

- `GET /v1/weather` also gives `tomorrow` (`max_temperature`, `min_temperature`, `rain_chance`, `sunrise`, `sunset`), shaped like `today`.
- New `PUT /v1/cameras/order` (admins) with `{"camera_ids": [...]}`; `GET /v1/cameras` and `GET /v1/doorbells` follow it; `features.camera_order`. Backups gain the optional `camera_order` section.
