# DirectorLink v1.1.1

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

Fixes for four rare cases around shades, found after 1.1.0.

## Fixed

- **Forgetting the key could say it no longer works.** **Forget access key** or **Pair again** in Settings → Controller, pressed while the app was following a shade (one moving, or sent a command in the last two minutes), could end on *This device’s access key no longer works*: the app went on checking the shade while the controller revoked the key. It now stops checking first.
- **After Stop, a shade could show *Opening…* again for a moment.** Control4 learns that a shade has stopped a moment after the Stop (on KNX shades, about a sixth of a second). A check on the shade in that moment still found it moving, and its line went back to *Opening…* (or *Closing…*) for up to 2 seconds. The app now shows the shade stopped until Control4 reports the stop.
- **A shade moving again after a while was checked on slowly.** When the app was in the background, or could not reach the controller, for more than two minutes while a shade moved, and the shade was moving again when the app looked again (moved from a keypad, say), the app checked on it every 10 seconds instead of every 2. It now follows it like any other move.
- **A shade could say *Moving…* for good.** After a controller restart, Control4 can leave a shade marked as not stopped while its position is not known. DirectorLink took that for a move that never ends: the shade said *Moving…*, and the app checked on it every 2 seconds, two minutes at a time. That now reads as not moving. It has not been seen on the test system.

## Installing

Update DirectorLink in Composer as usual (it loads without a reboot; keys, scenes and schedules stay). The app updates by itself. Nothing else changes.
