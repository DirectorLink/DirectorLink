# DirectorLink v1.1.1

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

Doors and gates are only pulsed now, and four rare cases around shades are fixed.

## Doors and gates are only pulsed

- DirectorLink opens doors and gates with a short press, like their Open button: the relay closes and opens again half a second later. Its API could also hold a relay closed (`PATCH /v1/relays/{id}` with `{"state": "closed"}`), which keeps a door unlocked or a gate open until someone releases it. Any key with door access could do that, at home or away. A new Composer property, **Relay Hold**, is **Not allowed** by default: holding a relay closed is then refused and nothing is sent. The app, scenes, `POST /v1/relays/{id}/pulse` and releasing a relay (`{"state": "open"}`) work exactly as before.
- If one of your scripts holds a relay closed on purpose (a relay that is not a door or gate), an installer can set Relay Hold to **Allowed**. It then applies to every relay, doors and gates included.

For developers: `PATCH /v1/relays/{id}` with `{"state": "closed"}` answers `409 HOLD_NOT_ALLOWED` unless Relay Hold is Allowed, and nothing is sent. A key's role and Door Control are still checked first (`403`). `{"state": "open"}` and pulses are unchanged. The API description's example body for this operation is now `{"state": "open"}`, which the console prefills.

## Fixed

- **Forgetting the key could say it no longer works.** **Forget access key** or **Pair again** in Settings → Controller, pressed while the app was following a shade (one moving, or sent a command in the last two minutes), could end on *This device’s access key no longer works*: the app went on checking the shade while the controller revoked the key. It now stops checking first.
- **After Stop, a shade could show *Opening…* again for a moment.** Control4 learns that a shade has stopped a moment after the Stop (on KNX shades, about a sixth of a second). A check on the shade in that moment still found it moving, and its line went back to *Opening…* (or *Closing…*) for up to 2 seconds. The app now shows the shade stopped until Control4 reports the stop.
- **A shade moving again after a while was checked on slowly.** When the app was in the background, or could not reach the controller, for more than two minutes while a shade moved, and the shade was moving again when the app looked again (moved from a keypad, say), the app checked on it every 10 seconds instead of every 2. It now follows it like any other move.
- **A shade could say *Moving…* for good.** After a controller restart, Control4 can leave a shade marked as not stopped while its position is not known. DirectorLink took that for a move that never ends: the shade said *Moving…*, and the app checked on it every 2 seconds, two minutes at a time. That now reads as not moving. It has not been seen on the test system.

## Installing

Update DirectorLink in Composer as usual (it loads without a reboot; keys, scenes and schedules stay). The app updates by itself. After the update DirectorLink has a new property, **Relay Hold**, set to `Not allowed`: leave it so unless a script holds relays closed. Nothing else changes.
