# DirectorLink v1.10.1

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

Alerts no longer go missing when the internet blinks, the app never gives up before the home answers, weather schedules follow one rule, and rooms no longer list parts of a door or doorbell as devices DirectorLink can't control.

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4 or Snap One.

## Alerts arrive when the connection blinks

If the doorbell rings, a camera sees someone or a door opens in the few seconds while the controller reconnects to DirectorLink's servers, the alert now arrives a few seconds late instead of never, and still only once (ADR-073). A doorbell ring or an ask-to-open question more than a minute late is dropped (the visitor has gone); other alerts are kept for up to two minutes. An ask-to-open question sent during a blink now reaches its person too.

## The app never gives up first

A tap that reaches the home while it is reconnecting gets its answer, or the relay's own "the home did not answer", within 18 seconds, before the app's 20 seconds run out. The app then says the home did not answer, instead of saying it could not reach DirectorLink's servers.

## A weather threshold itself counts

Weather schedules follow one rule: the threshold itself counts (ADR-074). "Only if 23° or warmer" now runs at exactly 23°, as "When it's 23° or hotter" always has, and "the wind is 20 km/h or less" runs at exactly 20 km/h. The app (in all four languages), Composer and the docs say "or hotter", "or more" and "or less". Existing schedules keep their numbers; only a condition at exactly its threshold, and a rule being ready again at exactly 2° (or 10 km/h) below it, behave differently.

## No more phantom devices in a room

A room no longer lists, among the "other devices" DirectorLink can't control:
- a gate's Control4 button, when the gate is a KNX relay that a Relay Gate, Door or Garage Door Controller drives (the gate shows once, under Doors & gates);
- a DoorBird's button and intercom (the DoorBird shows as the doorbell);
- an alarm partition that Home already shows.

## Installing

- **Deploy the cloud first** (it keeps working with DirectorLink 1.9.0 and 1.10.0 exactly as before, except that a request never waits more than 18 seconds), **then update DirectorLink in Composer** as usual. The app updates by itself. No D1 migration.
- **Going back to 1.10.0:** nothing is lost; nothing new is stored on the controller. Conditions compare strictly again at exactly their threshold.

## For developers

- **Relay (ADR-073, docs/RELAY.md):** one 18 s budget per relayed request, from its arrival at the home's object: the wait for the driver, sends, resends and answers all come out of it; the 504 `HOME_TIMEOUT` says how long it waited. The driver's `notify` frames carry an `id`; a relay that answers alerts says so with `relay_features` (`alert_acks`) after the hello, answers each with `notify_result`, and pushes an id once (kept 10 minutes in the home object's storage). The driver keeps unanswered alerts (at most 20, 128 KB; 60 s for rings and questions, 120 s for others) and sends them again after the next hello.
- **Devices:** every item of `/v1/devices` has `part_of`: the id of the door or doorbell a device is part of, while that one is shown and the caller may see it; null otherwise.
- **Schedules:** `only_if.hotter_than` is now `>=` and `only_if.wind_below` `<=`; a heat or wind rule is ready again at `<=` its way back. Field names are unchanged.
- **No D1 migration.** The Worker changes; deploy it first.
