# DirectorLink v1.14.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

Schedules you can plan: skip a run, dawn and dusk, earliest and latest times, running late after a power cut, your own holidays, and the next seven days at a glance.

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4 or Snap One.

## Skip the next run

Under each schedule, **Skip next run**: not tomorrow morning, and back to normal after it. The list says "Skipping tomorrow 06:45 · Next: Sun 06:45", **Don't skip** takes it back, and History says that someone skipped it. A weather rule can skip its next time too.

## Dawn and dusk, and not before or after a time

Sun schedules can now run at **dawn** or **dusk**, about half an hour before sunrise and after sunset, worked out on the controller like sunrise and sunset, without the internet. Any sun schedule can be held within a time: **at sunset, but not before 17:30**, or **at dawn, but not after 06:00**.

## Run late if missed

A time or sun schedule set to **Run late if missed** runs as soon as the controller is back after a power cut, a restart or an update, up to 6 hours late. Shabbat schedules always did.

## Your holidays

Schedules → **Holidays**: the days your home runs differently (public holidays, school breaks, a trip), once or every year. Each time, sun or weather schedule chooses **On your holidays: Run as usual / Not on holidays / Only on holidays**. They are separate from Shabbat and the Jewish holidays, which keep their own setting.

## The next 7 days

Schedules → **Next 7 days** shows each day's dawn, sunrise, sunset and dusk, your holiday, Shabbat and holidays, and every run at its time, or why a schedule won't run that day: skipped, a holiday, Shabbat, switched off, paused in Composer, or no sunset. Tap a run to open its schedule.

## Installing

- **Update DirectorLink in Composer** as usual. The app updates by itself; with an older controller it shows none of this.
- No Worker change and no D1 migration.
- **Going back to 1.13.2:** schedules at dawn or dusk, or with a not-before or not-after time, are left out by 1.13.2 and lost at its next change to a schedule; the others run as before, on every day and only 5 minutes late. A backup made by 1.14.0 is refused by 1.13.2, as for any newer backup.

## For developers

- `POST`/`DELETE /v1/schedules/{id}/skip`, `GET /v1/schedules/week`, `GET`/`PUT /v1/holidays` (admins); `features.schedule_planning`.
- Schedules: a sun trigger's `event` `dawn` and `dusk`, `earliest` and `latest`; `on_holidays`, `run_late` and `skip`; `last_run.skipped_by` `skip` and `holiday`. `GET /v1/weather` gives `dawn` and `dusk`. Backups have an optional `holidays` section; the history's `reason` may be `skip` or `holiday`.
- ADR-090; `docs/SCHEDULES.md` (Skipping the next run, Your holidays, The next seven days).
