# DirectorLink v1.12.2

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

Direct connection at home is switched in Composer only.

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4 or Snap One.

## Direct HTTPS is switched in Composer only

In 1.12.0 and 1.12.1 the direct connection at home took two switches: the installer allowed it in Composer, then the home's owner turned it on in the app. From 1.12.2 Composer is the only switch: DirectorLink → **Direct HTTPS** is Off (the default) or On, and On turns it on without anyone else having to approve. It still needs Remote Access and the home linked to an account, because the certificate comes through DirectorLink's servers; Direct HTTPS Status says so until then.

A home that had **Allowed** keeps Direct HTTPS on, with the same name and certificate, and Composer shows On after the update. A home whose owner had turned it off turns on. Settings → Controller → Direct connection at home still shows the state and what it means (the name is listed publicly in certificate logs, and DirectorLink's servers learn the name and the controller's home address), but has no switch; while it is off, admins see where the installer turns it on. History lists Direct connection at home turned on or off, by Composer (ADR-085).

## Installing

- **Update DirectorLink in Composer** as usual. The app updates by itself. The cloud is unchanged; no D1 migration.
- **Going back to 1.12.1** reads On as off: Direct HTTPS stops and its name is deleted. To have it again on 1.12.1, set Allowed in Composer and turn it on in that app.

## For developers

- `PUT /v1/https` answers `409 SET_IN_COMPOSER` for every admin. `GET /v1/https` keeps its shape: `allowed` and `enabled` both say Composer is On; `state` is `off`, `requesting`, `listening` or `error`.
- History `system` `direct_https` names Composer as who did it.
