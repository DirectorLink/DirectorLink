# DirectorLink v0.12.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

Your preferences follow you, on every device.

## Profiles

- Your **language, theme, colours, favorites and the rooms you hide** are now kept on the controller, in your profile, and shared by all your devices. Change the language on your phone and your computer follows within a minute.
- **Add my other device** puts the new phone in your profile: it opens in your language, with your favorites.
- The first time after the update, each device's own choices move into its profile, so nothing is lost.
- Two devices of one person that were paired separately: in **People and devices**, set one's *Person* to the other, and rename the person if you like.

## Rooms

- **Hide rooms for yourself:** Settings → Rooms, untick a room; it disappears from your Home and Climate, not from anyone else's.
- **One room order for the home:** admins move rooms up and down in Settings → Rooms; everyone sees the new order.

## Also

- Everything stays on your controller: nothing of it goes to DirectorLink's servers.
- The privacy page says where preferences are kept.

## Installing

Update DirectorLink in Composer (it loads without a reboot; keys and settings stay). The app updates by itself.

## Known issues

- An invitation has to be accepted with the email it was made for; letting the owner approve another address comes later.
- Only relay 1 of each KNX Contact/Relay device is controlled; KNX percentage dimming remains deferred (issue #11).
