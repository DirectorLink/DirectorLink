# DirectorLink v1.9.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

DirectorLink 1.9: users and their devices on one screen, say or type a command, and handing the home to another admin.

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4 or Snap One.

## Users and their devices

"People" are now **users**, on one screen: **Settings → Users** (ADR-061).

- **One user, one set of permissions** (Admin or Member, and a member's rooms, devices, cameras, doors and scenes), on all their devices. Two people who share one Google account are one user.
- **Their devices, under them.** Each user lists every phone and computer they connected, with when it was last used and **Remove**. Admins see every user; a member sees their own devices.
- **Up to 5 devices a user.** A sixth is refused with "Remove a device first" and the list of their devices.
- **Remote access with a Google or Apple account.** A user's devices reach the home from anywhere through their account. A user without an account works on the home network only: a child's iPad, a kitchen tablet, a script. An admin can invite the account of a user paired at home.
- **Devices of the same account become one user when an admin confirms.** Our servers say which devices share an account; Users then shows "These devices use the same account: make them one user?", with each user's role, and offers the one with less access. Nothing merges by itself, so our servers can never change anyone's access; if anything changed after you looked, nothing moves and the suggestion shows again. A merge that involves the owner is confirmed by the owner, who keeps their access. The moved devices take the kept user's access, language, theme and favorites.
- **Pairing at home is for a chosen user.** In Users, an admin taps **Pair a device** on a user (or **New user at home**) and gives the code to the new device. Composer's New Pairing Code still makes a new admin.
- **Members add and remove their own devices**: Add my other device (with the Google or Apple account their device already uses; an admin invites any other account), Join from another device approved on any device of their account, and Remove.

**When you update:** the users of 1.8.0 stay as they were. If one person's devices were split into two users (in 1.8.0 each device made its own), Users suggests making them one; tap it once.

## Say or type a command

On Home, type or say a simple sentence in English or Hebrew, with your own names (ADR-063):

| Say | Does |
|---|---|
| "kitchen lights off" · "כבו את האורות במטבח" | A room's lights off |
| "living room lights 30%" · "אור בסלון 40%" | A room's lights to a level (never the heaters wired as lights, unless you name one) |
| "living room AC to 23" · "מזגן בסלון 23" | The AC to 23 (asks the mode if it is off) |
| "close the kitchen blinds" · "kitchen blinds 40%" | Blinds |
| "run Good night" · "הפעילו לילה טוב" | A scene you may run |
| "pause music in the kitchen" · "kitchen volume 30" | Sonos |
| "turn off everything" · "כבו הכל" | Turn off all, after you confirm |
| "open the main gate" · "פתחו את השער" | The gate's button, waiting for your second tap |

- It is understood on your phone, without AI, and only for what you may control. It shows what it understood, then the result.
- It never guesses: when two names fit, or a short name is one letter off, it asks which one. A sentence with "?", a time ("at 7", "ב-7"), a change by an amount ("by 20%"), a feeling ("I'm cold", "חם לי") or "don't" does nothing; what it doesn't understand, it says so, with examples.
- Doors, gates and Turn off all still wait for your second tap.
- **The microphone** works where the browser has speech recognition (Chrome, Edge, Safari): the browser's own service turns speech into text (Chrome's Google, Edge's Microsoft, Safari's Apple). DirectorLink sends nothing anywhere. In the iPhone Home Screen app, use the keyboard's microphone.

## Hand the home to another admin

In **Settings → Users**, the home's owner can make another admin the owner (**Make Dana the owner**). Dana then alone changes their own access and devices, links the home to an account again, approves accounts that join with another email and replaces the home's secret. You stay an admin, and nobody is removed (ADR-064).

When the home is linked to an account, Dana's Google or Apple account becomes the home's account too: the confirm names it, Dana's account must have been invited to the home, and it is refused when it isn't clear which account is Dana's. If our servers don't answer in time, the hand-over is undone and you try again. Only the owner can hand the home over, and DirectorLink's servers follow only what the controller says.

## Ask before opening, and alerts

- **Only devices that can get the question are asked.** When a device's browser loses its alerts (iPhone drops them, or notifications are turned off), the app and our servers tell the controller. An ask-before-opening link then answers "nobody was asked" instead of "asked" (ADR-062).
- **The links of all your devices.** A door's Ask screen lists your other devices' links for that door, with Remove.

## Installing

- **Update DirectorLink in Composer** as usual: no reboot, and users, keys, scenes, schedules and settings stay. The app updates by itself.
- **Members:** open the app once after the update, so that you see requests to join from your own new devices (an older app gets the notification and shows nothing).
- **The Worker goes first, or with the driver:** members' Add my other device and undoing a hand-over need it.
- **Going back to 1.8.0:**
  - Users that were made one stay one; everyone keeps their devices and permissions.
  - 1.8.0 doesn't enforce 5 devices; an invitation an admin made for another user shows as the admin's own other device (it still joins that user).
  - A member's Add my other device invitation is refused by 1.8.0.
  - If the home was handed to another admin, 1.8.0 keeps them as the owner.

## For developers

- **New API routes:** `GET /v1/users`, `POST /v1/users/merge`, `POST /v1/users/owner`, `POST` and `DELETE /v1/pairing-code`.
- **Other API changes:**
  - `USER_DEVICE_LIMIT` (409) on every way a key joins a user, with `limit`, `user` and `devices`.
  - `profile_id` in `POST /v1/invitations`; members may make `for_me` invitations and remove their own user's keys (`DELETE /v1/api-keys/{id}`).
  - `GET /v1/ask-links` items have `device` and `this_user`.
  - `features.users` in `GET /v1/system`; History `access` entries for merges, suggestions and `owner_changed`.
  - A merge confirms a suggestion's `revision` (`409 SUGGESTION_CHANGED`); a member's `for_me` invitation is bound to an account of their device (`for_key`, `403 ACCOUNT_NOT_OF_DEVICE`); hand-over refusals `OWNER_NEEDS_ACCOUNT`, `OWNER_ACCOUNT_UNCLEAR`, `OWNER_ACCOUNT_SHARED`, `503 REMOTE_TIMEOUT`.
  - The API keeps its names (`/v1/profiles`, `profile_id`). Every change is additive; `api_version` stays `"1"`.
- **Relay protocol:** hello features `alerts_gone` and `users`; messages `accounts` (opaque per-home account tags of the keys), `alerts_gone`, and `owner`/`owner_result`/`owner_cancel`.
- **Cloud:** no D1 migration. Deploy the 1.9.0 Worker before or with the driver: a 1.8.0 Worker sends no account tags and refuses nothing new, but ignores a hand-over, which then times out and moves nothing.

## Known issues

- **Not yet seen on a real controller or phone:** the account tags and the first "make them one user" on the owner's home; Join from another device approved by a member; a hand-over through the account service; the microphone on Android and in Safari; Hebrew dictation on iPhone.
- **Commands** don't do two things in one sentence, or relative changes ("warmer", "a bit brighter").
- **The first ask after a device's alerts die** at the push service can still say "asked"; the next one says "nobody".
