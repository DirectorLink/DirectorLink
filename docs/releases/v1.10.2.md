# DirectorLink v1.10.2

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

Thermostats in °F as Control4 shows them, sensors that are just readings, "—" for what a device doesn't report, light switches without sliders, and KNX dimmers that dim.

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4 or Snap One.

## Thermostats in their own scale (#75)

There is no temperature setting: each thermostat shows in its own scale, as Control4 reports it, and everything not tied to one thermostat (the weather, weather schedules, "only if", refrigerators, Composer's printout) uses your project's scale (ADR-076). In a °F home the app shows whole degrees, steps by 1 °F and sends exactly the °F you choose: cards, Home, favorites, scenes, schedules and commands ("set the living room to 72", "2 degrees warmer"). °C homes look and work as before.

- **Separate heat and cool setpoints:** a thermostat with no single setpoint, such as a Nest, shows and sets the setpoint of its mode, with its own fan speeds, instead of −18°.
- **What a thermostat doesn't report shows as "—"**, never 0° or −18° (a floor-heating zone without a room temperature, a setpoint a driver doesn't give).
- **Temperature and humidity displays** that use the thermostat proxy show as one line ("Bathroom · 73° · 30%") with nothing to set, and weather drivers are left out of Climate.

## Switches are switches, and KNX dimmers dim (#11)

DirectorLink now asks each light's driver what it can do (ADR-077). A light whose driver says it only turns on and off (KNX switches among them) shows that way everywhere: no slider, On or Off on Home's favorites, no Dim in the scene editor, and "dim it to 50%" answers that it only turns on and off. A level now goes to every dimmer the way Control4 documents it, which is what the KNX dimmer's driver reads, so KNX dimmers dim from the app, scenes, schedules and commands. Scenes that set a level on a switch turn it on.

## Installing

- **Update DirectorLink in Composer** as usual. The app updates by itself. The cloud is unchanged; no D1 migration.
- **Going back to 1.10.1:** nothing is lost; nothing stored changes.
- Checked before release against a replay of a real °C home's scenes and Shabbat schedules: every command to its ACs, lights, blinds and lock the same as 1.10.1, apart from how a dimmer gets its level.

## For developers

- **Thermostats:** every thermostat gains `scale` ("C"/"F") and `sensor`, and `humidity` when reported; °F thermostats also get `*_f` fields (`current/target_temperature_f`, `heat/cool_setpoint_f`, `setpoint_deadband_f`, `target_temperature_min/max_f`), and PATCH accepts `target_temperature_f`, `heat_setpoint_f`, `cool_setpoint_f`. A value not reported is null. A sensor answers PATCH with 409 `NOT_SUPPORTED`. `GET /v1/system` gains `temperature_scale`.
- **Lights:** `dimmable` follows the light driver's declared capabilities (`<dimmer>`, `<set_level>`); a switch's PATCH with a brightness answers 409 `NOT_SUPPORTED`. A level is sent as `SET_BRIGHTNESS_TARGET` with `LIGHT_BRIGHTNESS_TARGET` and `RATE = 0`.
- Every API change is additive; `api_version` stays `"1"`. No Worker change.
