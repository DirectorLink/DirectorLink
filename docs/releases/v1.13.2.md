# DirectorLink v1.13.2

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

Signed in stays signed in while you use the app.

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4 or Snap One.

## Signed in stays signed in while you use the app

Until now, signing in to your DirectorLink account with Google or Apple lasted 30 days from the day you signed in, however much you used the app, and then the app quietly stopped reaching your home from away: at home everything still worked, so it looked like something was broken. From 1.13.2 your sign-in lasts 30 days from the last time you used the app, at home or away, so a phone you keep using stays signed in; one you leave unused for 30 days is signed out, as before, and once a year you are asked to sign in again. When the app is signed out, Home now says so: "You're signed out of your account, so the app can't reach your home away from it.", with the Sign in button (you can hide it until you next open the app), and away from home the app says you're signed out rather than that it can't reach your home. Sign out everywhere works as before (ADR-089).

## Installing

- **The cloud:** a Worker update, no D1 migration. Every app version benefits; only 1.13.2 shows the notice.
- **The app** updates by itself. **DirectorLink in Composer:** only its version number changes; update it when convenient.
- **Everyone signed out before the update** signs in once more (Settings → Account, or the notice on Home).

## For developers

- A session (`__Host-dl_session`) used a day or more after its last renewal ends 30 days later, at most a year after its sign-in; the answer sends the same cookie again with the new `Max-Age`. No API change.
