# DirectorLink v1.8.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

DirectorLink 1.8: admins and members, each member with their own rooms, devices and scenes; a gate that asks you before it opens; Sonos in several rooms at once and in scenes; camera pictures several at a time and camera alerts; and a few things DirectorLink now does by itself.

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4 or Snap One.

## Admins and members

Two roles, set per person instead of per device: all of a person's devices follow at once (ADR-054).

- **Admins** do everything, including making others admins.
- **Members** get what an admin chooses for them, in **Settings → People and devices → Change**:
  - **Rooms:** all, or a chosen list.
  - **Devices they use there:** lights, climate (AC and heating), fans, blinds, music, refrigerators. Each on or off; off hides that kind from them.
  - **Cameras:** the cameras in their rooms, and the picture at a doorbell.
  - **Doors and gates:** they always see those in their rooms; opening them needs this on, and Door Control on in Composer.
  - **Sees the alarm:** on at first.
  - **Scenes they may run:** chosen by an admin; such a scene runs in full. Members never change scenes, and don't see schedules, History or the controller's settings.
- **Hide a room from members** (Settings → Rooms): the room and its devices disappear for every member. Admins still see it, marked.
- **The controller enforces it**, on every request, not only in the app's screens. A device a member may not see answers exactly like one that does not exist.
- **The home's owner** (whoever linked the home to their account with 1.8.0, else the oldest admin) is always an admin: another admin can't demote them, change their access or revoke their devices, and only the owner can link the home to another account. There is always an admin.

**When you update,** everyone keeps what they had:

| Before (a device's role) | After (the person) |
| --- | --- |
| Admin | Admin |
| Doors | Member: every room and kind, cameras, doors and gates, the alarm, every scene |
| Member | Member: every room and kind, cameras, the alarm, every scene except those that open doors or gates |
| Viewer | Member with no rooms and cameras only: they see nothing until an admin gives them rooms |

A person with several devices gets the highest role among them.

## Ask before opening

A door or gate can have a link that asks instead of opening (ADR-058). In a room, a door's **Ask** makes it. Your phone's automation (an iPhone Arrive automation, Siri, an Android app) runs the link; DirectorLink then sends your own devices an encrypted notification, "Open Main gate?". Only your tap on **Open**, from your own device with its key, opens it.

- A leaked link can only make your phone ask, at most 10 times an hour. It never opens anything by itself.
- A question lasts two minutes and opens the door once, only from a device it was sent to.
- It needs alerts on the device that should be asked (on iPhone, the Home Screen app).
- A link stops working when its device's key is revoked, when its person may no longer open that door, or when the door is removed in Composer. **Remove All Scene Links** in Composer removes these links too.
- Every question and every opening is in History.

**Siri and Google Assistant:** scene links and these links now show how to start them by voice: an iPhone shortcut named like the scene ("Hey Siri, Good night"), or a Google Assistant routine that starts an Android app's request.

## Sonos in several rooms, and in scenes

- **Play in more rooms.** A playing room's **Play in more rooms…** adds other rooms, and they play the same music in step, as in the Sonos app. A room's **Play here too…** joins what plays elsewhere. **Leave group** takes one room out. The group has its own volume, which keeps the rooms' balance (ADR-057).
- **Music in scenes:** a scene (and so a schedule) can play a Sonos favorite in a room at a chosen volume, also in other rooms grouped with it, set a volume, or resume, besides pausing and stopping. Only favorites saved in Sonos.
- DirectorLink sends the players only two more actions (join a group, leave it), and still nothing about alarms or settings.

## Cameras

- **Pictures at the same time.** The app asks for 4 pictures at once instead of one after another, and the controller fetches up to 8 side by side (2 per camera, 3 per NVR). A camera's login is kept, so each picture is one request to the camera instead of two, and tiles of the same camera share one picture. In our tests (a simulated controller, 300 ms to reach the home), a grid of 11 cameras took about 3 s instead of 10 (ADR-055).
- **Camera alerts** from the free [DirectorLink · Hikvision](https://directorlink.io/drivers/hikvision) drivers: "Person at Garden at 21:14", sealed like every alert, to those who may see that camera and chose it in **Settings → Controller → Alerts on this device**. Off at first; what each camera alerts about is its Alert On in Control4; at most one a minute per camera. Tapping opens the camera (ADR-056).

## Things DirectorLink does by itself

- **A device whose driver is updated is set up again.** When you update a device's driver in Composer, DirectorLink notices within a few minutes and reads that device again, so nothing new is missed until a Refresh Project (for example the refrigerator driver's list of what the model has).
- **Favorites of removed devices** show as "Removed in Composer" with **Remove**, instead of an empty tile. After 7 days DirectorLink removes them itself.
- **A notification when a new device asks to join**, on admins' devices with alerts on, so the approving device need not have the app open.
- **Older drivers can be turned away.** If a flaw is ever found in the remote connection, our servers can refuse drivers without the fix until they are updated; the app and Composer then say "Update DirectorLink". Nothing is refused today, and the home network is never affected (ADR-059).

## Installing

- **Update DirectorLink in Composer** as usual: no reboot, and keys, scenes, schedules and settings stay. The app updates by itself.
- **Check People and devices** after the update: former viewers see nothing until you give them rooms, and members without doors see their doors but can't open them.
- **Going back to 1.7.0:**
  - Every device keeps a 1.7.0 role worked out from its person (admin; a member with doors: doors; a member with no rooms: viewer; else member), so 1.7.0 behaves close to 1.8.0. But 1.7.0 doesn't limit rooms, kinds, cameras or scenes: a member controls every room again.
  - Ask-before-opening links stop until 1.8.0 is back; music steps other than pause and stop are left out of scenes while 1.7.0 runs, and come back with 1.8.0 unless a scene was changed.
  - Camera alert choices are dropped by 1.7.0: choose them again after updating.
  - A 1.8.0 backup doesn't restore on 1.7.0.

## For developers

- **New API routes:**
  - `GET` and `PATCH /v1/profiles/{profileId}/access`; `PATCH /v1/rooms/{roomId}` takes `hidden_from_members`;
  - `GET`, `POST /v1/ask-links`, `DELETE /v1/ask-links/{linkId}`; `POST /v1/relays/{id}/pulse` takes `request`;
  - `POST`, `PATCH`, `DELETE /v1/music/{id}/group`.
- **Other API changes:**
  - **Roles:** routes are `member` or `admin`; the handlers answer with what the caller may see (a device a member may not see is `404`).
  - **What a key may do:** `GET /v1/api-keys/current`, `/v1/profile` and `/v1/profiles` gain `access`. `POST /v1/invitations` and `/v1/api-keys` take `access`.
  - **Admins only:** `GET /v1/schedules` and `/v1/schedules/{id}`.
  - **Music:** `GET /v1/music` has `groups`; music scene steps `resume`, `volume` and `play_favorite`.
  - **Profiles:** `gone_favorites` in `GET` and `PATCH /v1/profile`.
  - **System:** `GET /v1/system` `features` gains `people_permissions`, `ask_links`, `sonos_groups` and `camera_alerts`.
  - **Alerts:** the alert kinds `camera` and `device_requests`.
  - **History:** `access` `role_changed`, `permissions_changed`, `room_hidden`, `room_shown`, the ask link entries, `door` `asked`, `system` `remote_update_required`.
  - **Backups:** a `people` section.
  - Every change is additive; `api_version` stays `"1"`. A member's 1.7.0 app gets `403` on Schedules.
- **Relay protocol:** the `notify` kinds `open_request` (brief) and `camera`; `link_result` result words for ask links.
- **Cloud:**
  - `MIN_DRIVER_VERSION` (unset: no minimum), `426 DRIVER_UPDATE_REQUIRED` on `/relay/connect`, `503 HOME_UPDATE_REQUIRED`;
  - the device request push, and the `device_requests` choice with a push registration.
  - No D1 migration. Deploy the 1.8.0 Worker before or with the 1.8.0 driver: a 1.7.0 Worker doesn't know the ask links' results.

## Known issues

- **Not yet seen on a real controller or phone:**
  - an ask link run by an iPhone Arrive automation, and the question's tap opening the Home Screen app on its screen;
  - Siri and Google Assistant starting a link;
  - Sonos grouping and favorites in scenes on real players;
  - the Hikvision driver's Alert reaching DirectorLink, and several kept camera logins at once on a Hikvision camera or NVR;
  - a device set up again after its driver is updated.
- **Ownership** can't be handed to someone else yet: Composer's Revoke All API Keys is the way, and the next admin paired becomes the owner.
- **Scripts with a viewer key** see nothing after the update until an admin gives that person rooms and kinds.
- **A Sonos room joined** to a group stays grouped until someone takes it out, in the app or the Sonos app.
