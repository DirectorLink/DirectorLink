# DirectorLink v1.4.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

DirectorLink 1.4: a password-protected backup of everything DirectorLink keeps.

## Backup and restore

Updating DirectorLink keeps everything it knows. But removing the driver from the project, even by accident, replacing the controller, or rebuilding the project lost it all. Now admins can keep a backup (ADR-042, docs/BACKUP.md).

- **Download backup**, in Settings → Controller → Backup, saves *DirectorLink backup <home> <date>.dlbackup*.
  - **What's in it:** scenes, schedules, each person's preferences and favorites, room names and the room order, the Shabbat settings, which devices have access, and the link to your account.
  - **The password:** you choose it and type it twice (at least 10 characters; the hint flags common passwords and patterns). The file is locked with it in your browser. Without it, nobody can open the file, not even DirectorLink. Keep the file away from the controller, and the password somewhere else.
- **Restore from backup** opens the file with its password and shows what it holds before anything changes:
  - the date and the counts;
  - the keys that come back, by name and role;
  - devices found by name in a rebuilt project;
  - anything no longer in the project, with the scenes and favorites that used it;
  - how the Composer properties are set, which a restore doesn't change.

  **Replace everything** then puts it all back at once, or nothing if the controller can't save it.
- **Keys come back only right after a reinstall**, when only the restoring device has paired. Otherwise every key stays as it is now, so a key revoked or demoted since the backup never returns.
  - **This device is …** lets the restoring device take its old key's place, with that person's favorites and role.
- **Another home's backup is recognised.** It's named in a warning and in the confirmation. Its remote access moves here only if you tick **Move remote access to this controller**, and the other controller must then be off or reset.
- **Remote access and the family's accounts come back with the backup**, when the backup's link was in use. If DirectorLink's servers no longer accept that link, the controller keeps its own, and Remote Status says so.
- **Doors and gates are never moved** to another device or room by a restore. Other swapped devices and rooms follow their names.
- **What a restore never touches:**
  - Composer properties (Door Control, Relay Hold, Alarm Status, Remote Access…).
  - Old schedule runs: schedules start from the restore, so nothing due before it runs.
  - Invitations not yet accepted: they are cancelled.
- **For admins only**, and only through the app's sealed connection, at home or through the account.
- **Restore before the family opens the app.** A phone that opened it while the controller's data was gone has to pair again.
- Backup appears only with DirectorLink 1.4.0 or newer on the controller.

## For developers

- **Backup** (admins, sealed requests only; plain requests get `403 SEALED_REQUEST_REQUIRED`):
  - `GET /v1/backup`, which now holds `controller_id` and `remote_identity.linked`;
  - `POST /v1/restore/parts`;
  - `POST /v1/restore`: a dry run by default; `"dry_run": false` restores; it takes `replaces_key` and `move_remote`.
  - The preview has `origin`, `keys`, `remote` and `unmatched`.
  - New codes: `KEYS_KEPT`, `LAST_ADMIN`, `UNAVAILABLE`.
  - `GET /v1/system` has `features.backup: true`; drivers before 1.4.0 don't say it, and the app shows them no Backup.
- Every change is additive; `api_version` stays `"1"`. The cloud is unchanged.

## Installing

- Update DirectorLink in Composer as usual (no reboot; keys, scenes and schedules stay). The app updates by itself.
- No stored data changes format.
- **Going back to 1.3.0:**
  - Everything reads the same, but a 1.4.0 backup can't be restored there.
  - After a restore that moved remote access from another controller, go back only once Remote Status says *Connected*.

## Known issues

- 1.4.0 was tested against a fake Director, not yet on a real controller.
  - A restore of a home at DirectorLink's limits is estimated at a few seconds on a CORE-1.
  - How the controller is told apart from another one (its MAC address) hasn't been seen on a real controller yet; the *backup made* log line says whether it was known.
- Through the account, a very large backup may take longer than the relay waits (15 s). Download big backups at home.
