# DirectorLink v1.14.1

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

Next 7 days shows only what may run.

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4 or Snap One.

## Next 7 days, only what may run

Schedules → Next 7 days listed every schedule's time each day, including the ones that wouldn't run, with the reason. A schedule set not to run on Shabbat showed on Saturday morning as "Not on Shabbat and holidays", which looked like a run. From 1.14.1 those runs are left out: the day's Shabbat line still says when Shabbat begins or ends.

Weather rules are listed only on days their weather may come. DirectorLink checks the forecast it keeps for your home over the rule's hours that day, at the times its Shabbat setting allows: a "hotter than 23°" rule on a day forecast to stay at 20° is no longer listed. The forecast reaches 5 days ahead, so a day further on is listed until it does, and a rule that ran today stays listed today. The forecast is read again every 6 hours, so a day can come back, or go.

The schedules themselves run exactly as before.

## Installing

- **DirectorLink in Composer:** update it to 1.14.1. No Worker change and no D1 migration.
- **The app** updates by itself. With an older controller it still leaves out the runs skipped for Shabbat; weather rules are left out only with 1.14.1.

## For developers

- `GET /v1/schedules/week` lists a weather rule's day only while the saved forecast says its weather may come in its hours (from now on, today) at a moment its `during_shabbat` allows, or when the forecast doesn't reach them, or on a day the rule ran. Runs `skipped` with `shabbat` are still listed; the app hides them (ADR-090, amended).
