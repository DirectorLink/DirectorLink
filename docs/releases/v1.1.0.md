# DirectorLink v1.1.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

DirectorLink 1.1: older Control4 lights, floor heating that is set through its heat setpoint, and Control4 thermostats with separate heat and cool setpoints, thanks to @bkwagner, who found them in his own house and sent the changes (#14, #19, #16). They were rebuilt on 1.0.0. The controller also checks the certificate of DirectorLink's relay now. Shades get only the controls they can use and show where they are going while they move, changes made in Composer reach the app without restarting DirectorLink, and the room order works again. Admins now see in the app when a new version is out.

## Older Control4 lights

- Dimmers and switches on Control4's older Light proxy (`light.c4i`, such as the LDZ-101/102 and LDZ-5S1) were listed as unsupported. They now work like any other light: on, off, and brightness for dimmers, in the app, the API and scenes. In the house where this was found, that was 25 of 38 lights.
- A dimmer goes to a new level at its own rate, as set by the installer.

## Floor heating that uses its heat setpoint

- Some heat-only thermostats (Thermostat V2) keep their target in the heat setpoint and leave the single setpoint at 0. DirectorLink showed their target as −18° and sent them a command they do not have. It now shows the real target and sets the heat setpoint, down to 5°, since floor heating is often parked low.
- This happens only when the zone has no Cool or Auto mode and its single setpoint reads 0. It is checked again whenever those values change, so a zone that reads 0 for a moment during a restart goes back when the real value comes. Air conditioners, and zones whose single setpoint is in use, work exactly as before.
- A fan mode the thermostat reports as *Undefined* is no longer shown as a fan speed.

## Thermostats with heat and cool setpoints

- Control4 thermostats with separate heat and cool setpoints (`control4_thermostat_proxy.c4i`, such as Control4's wireless thermostat) were unsupported. They now show in Climate and Home with the modes they have, of Off, Heat, Cool and Auto.
- In **Heat** or **Cool**, the card has one stepper for that setpoint. In **Auto** it has two, **Heat** and **Cool** (one under the other on a phone), and Home shows the range, such as *Auto 20°–24°*.
- The thermostat keeps a smallest gap between the two (its deadband). Raising heat into it moves cool up with it, and lowering cool moves heat down; the stepper stops where the other one has no room left.
- In a °F project DirectorLink sends whole degrees Fahrenheit, as the thermostat takes them, so a setting can come back up to 0.3° off (22.5° is sent as 73 °F and shows as 22.8°). The app counts that as confirmed.
- New fan speeds: **On** and **Circulate**, where the thermostat has them.

## Shades

- **A slider only where it works.** Shades that can go to a position, such as KNX shades with a Percent Set Address, keep their slider. Shades that only open and close fully get **Open** and **Close** without a slider: before, 50% on such a shade opened it all the way. DirectorLink reads this from the shade's Control4 setup, as Control4's own apps do. **Stop** is shown only on shades that can stop.
- **You see the shade move.** After Open, Close or a new position, the line under the shade's name says *Opening… to 53%* (or *Opening…* and *Closing…* when it goes all the way), the slider stays where the shade is going, and the app checks on it every 2 seconds. When the shade stops, the app shows where it is, and checks a few seconds more for the position the shade's actuator reports after the stop. After **Stop** it shows the shade stopped at once. Before, the slider jumped back to the old position for most of a move, which takes 10 to 70 seconds. A shade moved from a keypad or the Control4 app shows its movement too, and one that never stops saying it moves is checked on every 2 seconds for two minutes at most.
- A shade whose position is not known (its actuator reports none, or has not yet since a restart) says *Position unknown*.
- A shade whose Control4 driver counts its levels other than 0 to 100 (0 closed and 2 open, say) shows and takes positions from 0 to 100 like every other shade, from the Closed and Open levels of its Control4 setup.
- Scene actions that set a position between 0 and 100 leave out shades that only open and close fully, and the run says so; Open and Close work on every shade. The scene editor offers **Set position** only when one of the shades chosen can use it, and **Copy the house as it is now** gives such a shade Open or Close, whichever it is nearer to.

## Changes in Composer are picked up

- A device moved to another room, renamed, added or removed in Composer, and rooms added or removed, are meant to reach the app without restarting DirectorLink: DirectorLink asks Director to tell it about Composer's changes, and reads the project again 5 seconds after the last one (after a long run of changes, at most 30 seconds after the first). This has not yet run on a real controller; the log says whether Director announces the changes (docs/TESTING.md, 0r). A read that fails, for example while Director loads a project, is tried again a minute later.
- New Composer action **Refresh Project** does it at once, for example when a change has not shown after a minute. Composer's **Refresh Navigators** does it too, at most every two minutes (Director also sends it when bindings, names or media change, and one read of a large home is over a thousand Director calls); one that comes sooner is read at the end of the two minutes.
- Scenes, schedules, favorites, room names and the room order stay as they are: they refer to devices and rooms by their Control4 id. A scene action for a room acts on the devices in that room when it runs; a device that was removed is skipped, and the run says so. The app shows the change at its next refresh (rooms, and the devices it cannot control, within a minute).
- What a refresh reads again about each device is logged at debug level only, so that the log (500 lines) keeps its older lines, door openings among them.

## Room order works again

- Moving rooms up or down in Settings → Rooms works again, at home and away. Since 1.0.0 it failed with *Remote requests are GET, POST, PATCH or DELETE on /v1/...* (a bug in 1.0.0). Until the controller has 1.1.0, the app says to update DirectorLink there instead.

## Remote access: the relay's certificate is checked

- The controller now checks the certificate of DirectorLink's relay (`api.directorlink.io`) before it sends the home's secret, against the certificate authorities that Cloudflare issues it from, which come with the driver. Before, it checked nothing: someone in the network path between the controller and the internet could pose as the relay, take the secret and keep the home offline. They could never read or change anything, because everything through the relay is sealed end to end.
- An attempt to reach the relay that has not connected within 30 seconds is now given up and tried again. Before, it could stay at *Connecting...* until the driver restarted.

## New versions in the app

- Admins now see in the app when a new DirectorLink version is out: **Settings → Controller** shows it with **What's new**, a download of that release's `DirectorLink.c4z` and the steps in Composer, and Home shows a short notice until it is dismissed for that version. Members don't see it, and the driver is still updated in Composer. For this, the admin's app asks GitHub about twice a day which version is the newest; GitHub sees the device's IP address, and nothing about the home is sent.

## Scenes and schedules

- **Auto with heat and cool.** In the scene editor, Auto on thermostats with heat and cool setpoints offers a Heat and a Cool stepper (they keep the gap of those thermostats). **Copy the house as it is now** keeps both setpoints of a thermostat in Auto.
- A target temperature in a scene sets the setpoint of the scene's mode on these thermostats, so an existing *Cool 24°* scene works on them too. In Auto they need heat and cool: a target alone is left out, and the run says so.
- A setpoint the thermostat refuses (closer to the other one than its deadband) is left out and the run says so; the rest of the scene runs. A thermostat left with nothing to do is skipped.
- **Check your room and whole-home scenes.** Actions for "all lights" or "all AC" in a room or the whole home include every light and thermostat DirectorLink supports when the scene runs. After this update, scenes such as *All off*, *Good night* or *Leaving*, and the schedules that run them, also switch the newly supported lights and thermostats. The room's **All off** button and the counts on Home include them too. Composer's **Inventory** shows how many there are now.

## For developers

- Every thermostat has four new fields: `setpoints` (`single` or `dual`), `heat_setpoint`, `cool_setpoint` and `setpoint_deadband`, in °C. They are `single` and `null` on the thermostats DirectorLink supported before, and every other field stays as it was.
- On a dual thermostat, `target_temperature` is the setpoint of the current mode, and `null` in auto and off. A setpoint none of its modes uses (heat on one with only Off and Cool) is `null` too.
- `PATCH /v1/thermostats/{id}` takes `heat_setpoint` and `cool_setpoint`. One setpoint moves the other when needed to keep the deadband; both together must already be that far apart (`400 INVALID_FIELD`). Without a reported deadband, cool must still be above heat. With `target_temperature` too the answer is `400 INVALID_REQUEST`, and on a single-setpoint thermostat `409 NOT_SUPPORTED`. `target_temperature` sets the setpoint of the request's mode (else the current one) and is refused in auto and off (`409`). Nothing is sent when a request is refused. See `api/README.md`, *Thermostats*.
- Scene steps take `heat_setpoint` and `cool_setpoint` (5–40, cool above heat, not with `target_temperature`).
- Fan speeds gain `on` and `circulate`, and `fan_speeds` lists only the speeds the API defines.
- Clients made for 1.0.0 keep working: on a dual thermostat `target_temperature` works in heat and cool; in auto it is `null` and a change is refused with `409`.
- Blinds have four new fields: `capabilities` (`position`, `stop`), `moving` (`null` when the controller does not report movement), `direction` (`opening`, `closing` or `null`) and `target_position`. A blind with `capabilities.position` false takes only 0 and 100 (`409 POSITION_NOT_SUPPORTED` for anything else, and nothing is sent); stopping one with `capabilities.stop` false is `409 STOP_NOT_SUPPORTED`. Where the controller does not say, both are true and blinds work as before. `moving` and `direction` come from the blind proxy's Opening, Closing and Stopped variables, and positions are 0 to 100 whatever levels the shade's driver uses. See `api/README.md`, *Blinds*.
- Sealed requests (the app's, at home and through the account) accept `PUT`, so `PUT /v1/rooms/order` works for the app again. `scripts/check_package.py` fails the build when a method the API routes is refused there.

## Installing

Update DirectorLink in Composer (it loads without a reboot; keys, scenes and schedules stay). The app updates by itself.

Going back to 1.0.0 keeps keys, scenes and schedules, but not every scene action survives it:
- An action with heat and cool setpoints and a mode runs with the mode only.
- An action with setpoints and no mode is left out.
- An action with fan speed **On** or **Circulate** is left out entirely, with its mode and target temperature.

An action that is left out is gone for good once the scene is saved on 1.0.0. The older lights and the thermostats with heat and cool setpoints are unsupported again, and floor heating that uses its heat setpoint shows −18° again.

## Known issues

- The three new device families are covered by tests against the fake Director. Their variable IDs, values and command lists were read on the contributor's Director over Director REST; no DirectorLink command has run on these devices yet.
- °C projects are less proven still: the contributor's project is °F, and his floor-heating zone lists only the `FAHRENHEIT` form of its heat setpoint command. In a °C project DirectorLink sends `CELSIUS`, both to floor heating set through its heat setpoint and to thermostats with heat and cool setpoints. A floor-heating zone that showed −18° before now uses that path.
- Thermostats with heat and cool setpoints are kept within 5–35 °C; DirectorLink does not read limits of their own. Modes other than Off, Heat, Cool and Auto (such as Emergency Heat) are not offered.
- An older light that ramps to a new level slower than about 4 seconds, or whose driver does not report its level, shows *Sent — waiting for the device to confirm* after dimming even when it ends up right.
- Room and whole-home scene actions now include the newly supported lights and thermostats (see *Scenes and schedules*).
- Going back to 1.0.0 drops heat and cool setpoints from scene actions, and leaves out whole actions that use fan speed On or Circulate (see *Installing*).
- What a shade can do comes from Control4's `GET_SETUP` request, whose answer is not documented; a controller that does not answer it leaves every shade with its slider and Stop, and positions from 0 to 100, as before. Whether a shade moves is read from the proxy's Opening, Closing and Stopped variables (Movement is only the kind of movement, such as *Up to Down*). Their IDs were read on Director 3.4.3, but their values during a move, what Director sends when a device is moved in Composer, and whether it announces Composer's changes at all, have not yet been seen on a real controller; with **Log Level** Debug the log shows them (docs/TESTING.md, 0r). After a command, a shade that reports its position but not its movement (as with DirectorLink 1.0.0) shows *Opening…* or *Closing…* until it reaches the position or its position stops changing, and one that reports neither for up to two minutes.
- If Cloudflare moves the relay to a certificate authority that is not in the driver, remote access stops until DirectorLink is updated. Control4 does not say whether Director also checks the name on the certificate.
- Someone who can change traffic on your home network during pairing (not only listen) could still take that key; pair on a network you trust. Local HTTPS would close this.
- The size of a sealed message still shows roughly what it is (a camera picture or a command).
- The API console (console.directorlink.io) still sends its key openly on the home network: use it on a network you trust.
- A Composer schedule and a DirectorLink schedule that act on the same device both run; DirectorLink does not read Composer programming.
- Only relay 1 of each KNX Contact/Relay device is controlled; KNX percentage dimming remains deferred (issue #11).
