# DirectorLink v0.15.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

DirectorLink's automation is now visible to the installer, in Composer.

## For installers

New on the DirectorLink device in Composer:

- **Schedules** — `On` or `Paused`. Pausing stops every DirectorLink schedule at once, without deleting anything; handy while troubleshooting. The app tells the household the installer paused them.
- **Schedule Status** — what is on and what runs next, e.g. `3 on · next tomorrow 06:45 Good morning · 1 weather rule`.
- **Last Automation** — the last scene DirectorLink ran, when, and why: `28 Sep 13:10 Cool the house · heat rule, 31C outside · 22 devices`, or `Good night · run from Dana's iPhone`. So "the AC turned off and nobody knows why" has an answer in Composer.
- **Print Schedules and Scenes** (action) — lists every schedule and scene, with each step and the device names and ids, in the Lua output.

Scenes and schedules are DirectorLink's own; they do not appear in Composer programming, and these properties are where to find them.

## Installing

Update DirectorLink in Composer (it loads without a reboot; everything stays). The app updates by itself.

## Known issues

- A Composer schedule and a DirectorLink schedule that act on the same device both run; DirectorLink does not read Composer programming.
- Only relay 1 of each KNX Contact/Relay device is controlled; KNX percentage dimming remains deferred (issue #11).
