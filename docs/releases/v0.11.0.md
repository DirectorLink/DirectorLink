# DirectorLink v0.11.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

Who has access to your home, in the app.

## People and devices

- **Settings → Controller → People and devices** (admin keys): every access key of the controller with its role, when it was last used and a **Revoke** button; change a device's role right there. The device you are using is marked *This device*.
- The invitations waiting to be accepted, and who made each: **Revoke** one and its link stops working.
- For the home's owner, the people who reach the home through their account, with their devices. **Remove** revokes their keys at home and then ends their membership.

## Membership follows the keys

- Revoking a device's key, in the app, in the API console or with Composer's **Revoke All API Keys**, now also ends that account's membership of the home when it was its last key. Before, the account stayed listed until the owner removed it.
- The controller tells DirectorLink's servers only the ids of its keys, never names, roles or the keys themselves.

## Also

- Sign in with Apple is built and waits for its keys; the app shows it once it is set up.
- Accounts are found only by the provider's own id, never by email.
- Invitations may be accepted with the email of any of the account's sign-ins.
- The privacy page describes remote access and Apple as they are.

## Installing

Update DirectorLink in Composer (it loads without a reboot; keys and settings stay). The app updates by itself.

## Known issues

- An invitation has to be accepted with the email it was made for; letting the owner approve another address comes later.
- Pictures through the account refresh every few seconds, not continuously.
- Only relay 1 of each KNX Contact/Relay device is controlled; KNX percentage dimming remains deferred (issue #11).
