# DirectorLink v0.11.1

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

A fix for air conditioning.

## Fixed

- **An AC could refuse its own temperature.** DirectorLink allowed air-conditioning zones 16–25 °C, although Control4 allows them up to 32 °C (an AC that is off is often left at 32 °C). Such an AC showed 32 °C, and a change sent back from there was refused ("target_temperature must be a number from 16 to 25"). The range is now 16–32 °C for every zone, as in Control4.
- **A temperature tap could be lost.** Changing the fan or the mode and then tapping − or + right away could send the old temperature instead of the one chosen, when the first change was confirmed in between. The app now always sends the temperature you chose.

## Installing

Update DirectorLink in Composer (it loads without a reboot; keys and settings stay). The app updates by itself.
