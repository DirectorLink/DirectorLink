# DirectorLink v0.10.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

Your home from anywhere, on iPhone too, locked end to end.

## Remote access with your account

- **Sign in with Google** in the app (Settings → Account). Signing in is optional: on the home network the app keeps working without an account.
- **Link your home once**, from a computer or an Android phone at home: Settings → Account → **Link this home to my account**. It needs **Remote Access** = On in Composer.
- After that the app reaches your home from anywhere. When the home network cannot be reached it switches to your account by itself, and back once you are home; the status chip then says **Connected · via account**.
- **Every request and answer is locked end to end**, with a key that only your device and your controller hold. DirectorLink's servers pass them on but cannot read or change them, and a captured request cannot be sent again.
- Each device keeps its own access key and role (viewer, member, doors, admin), and Door Control still applies.

## iPhone, iPad and family

- **Add my other device**: a QR code, valid for 10 minutes and working once. Scan it with your iPhone, sign in with the same account and accept.
- **Invite someone**: an email address and a role. The link works once, for 7 days, and only for that email. The invitation's secret stays in the link and never reaches DirectorLink's servers.
- On iPhone and iPad the app always uses the account connection, because iOS blocks the home-network connection for web apps.

## Installing

1. Update DirectorLink in Composer (it loads without a reboot; keys and settings stay).
2. Set **Remote Access** to On in DirectorLink's properties.
3. On a computer at home: open the app, sign in with Google, then Settings → Account → **Link this home to my account**, and **Add my other device** for your phone.

At every start the driver checks that the controller's encryption works as expected; the log shows `lock self-test passed`. If it fails, remote requests are refused rather than weakened.

## Security

- The relay can no longer read a home: the plain relayed requests of the 0.9.2 test are refused (`410 RELAY_REQUESTS_RETIRED`), and the test endpoints are off.
- A captured request cannot be replayed, not even after the driver restarts.
- Revoking a key revokes the invitations it made; **Revoke All API Keys** in Composer also revokes every invitation and claim token.
- An invitation is bound to its email once; nobody can move it to another address.
- An account session that ends (sign-out, expiry) never makes the app forget the device's key; the app offers to sign in again.
- When the home network drops in the middle of a command, the command is not sent a second time through the account, so a gate never opens twice.
- The app returns to the home network only after the controller there has answered as this home, with this device's key.
- **Link this home** asks before taking over a home that belongs to another account.

## Known issues

- Sign in with Apple comes later; Google only for now.
- An invitation has to be accepted with the email it was made for; letting the owner approve another address comes later.
- Revoking a device's key ends its access at once, but its account stays listed as a member of the home until the owner removes it.
- Pictures through the account refresh every few seconds, not continuously.
- Only relay 1 of each KNX Contact/Relay device is controlled; KNX percentage dimming remains deferred (issue #11).
