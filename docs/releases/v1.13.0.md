# DirectorLink v1.13.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

Alerts on the home's TVs, and a direct connection at home that guards its name.

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4 or Snap One.

## Alerts on the home's TVs

DirectorLink now sends its alerts to the home's TVs and other screens. Any screen driver that follows DirectorLink's new notification agreement (docs/NOTIFY_DRIVERS.md), such as the coming DirectorLink · LG TV Notifications driver, gets a pop-up for the doorbell, camera alerts (a smoke or CO alarm a camera hears as an alarm), doors and gates opened, the refrigerator door left open and schedules that had a problem. The doorbell and cameras come with a picture. The words are in the language the home's owner sees in the app, the same sentences as on the family's phones, and they come straight from the controller: no phone needs alerts switched on, and the internet can be down. The installer adds the screen driver and chooses what each screen shows in that driver's properties; DirectorLink has nothing new to set. Admins see the screens in Settings → Alerts → On TVs (ADR-086).

With Alarm Status on in Composer, the TVs also show when the home's alarm goes off ("Fire alarm at House at 21:14."), once each time, and nothing else of the alarm: never armed or disarmed. It stays in the home: no phone gets it, and nothing of it reaches the internet.

## A direct connection at home that guards its name

Direct HTTPS (1.12) gets three improvements, none of which changes how it is used (ADR-087):
- **Only DirectorLink's own certificate account** can get certificates for dlhome.cc names, once its CAA record is set: a stranger can't get one for your home's name, even with Let's Encrypt.
- **Names nobody uses are cleaned up** every day: a home that no longer exists loses its name, and a controller that turned it off or hasn't been seen for 30 days loses its DNS record (its name stays its own for when it comes back).
- **No more warning at start** when Director restarts the driver's servers during an update; a real outage still warns.

## Installing

- **The cloud first:** a Worker update with one D1 migration (`0012_https_cleanup`). Older drivers work with it.
- **Update DirectorLink in Composer** as usual. The app updates by itself.
- **Going back to 1.12.2:** nothing is lost; screens simply stop getting alerts. A backup made by 1.13.0 is refused by 1.12.2, as for any newer backup.

## For developers

- The notification agreement v1: a screen driver sets `DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY` = "1" and `DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY_KINDS`, and takes the command `DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY` (`VERSION`, `ID`, `KIND`, `TITLE`, `MESSAGE`, `PICTURE`). New `GET /v1/alerts/screens` (admins) and `GET /v1/notify-pictures/{picture}` (a 60-second, three-use picture link); `features.alert_screens`.
- `PATCH /v1/profile` takes `prefs.shown_language`, the language an app shows (reported by the app; the screens' language when the owner chose Auto).
- The Worker keeps its Let's Encrypt account URL in D1 (`acme_accounts`) for the CAA record, and runs a daily clean-up of unused names (`https_names_cleaned`).
