# DirectorLink v1.6.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

DirectorLink 1.6: a history of what happened, alerts on your phone, automatic backups to your account, scene actions you can change, and a steadier remote connection.

## History

Admins can see what the controller did and noticed in the last 30 days, in **Settings → Controller → History** (ADR-046, docs/HISTORY.md).

- **Scenes and schedules:** what ran, and what didn't, with the reason: Shabbat or a holiday, schedules paused in Composer, the Jewish calendar off, the weather, no weather data.
- **Doors and gates:** what was opened, and by whom.
- **Access:** keys paired, added, changed, removed or expired; invitations accepted.
- **Changes in Composer:** devices and rooms removed, added, renamed or moved, by name, including devices DirectorLink doesn't control (a keypad button, for example), and DirectorLink's settings changed there.
- **Backups and the system:** backups (by hand and automatic), restores, DirectorLink updates and restarts, and remote access down for more than a minute.
- **How it's shown:** grouped by day, with filters. The controller keeps it across restarts and updates. It isn't in backups.

## Alerts on your phone and computer (admins)

Turn on **Settings → Controller → Alerts on this device** (ADR-047). You get a notification, even with the app closed, when:
- your home has been **offline for 10 minutes** (short drops of a few seconds never alert);
- **a schedule had a problem** (a device didn't respond, or it couldn't run).

Tapping an alert opens History. Alerts never name a room, device or schedule: they say "Your home" and the time.

You need an account linked to the home, and Remote Access on. On iPhone and iPad, add DirectorLink to the Home Screen first (iOS 16.4 or later).

## Automatic backups to your account

Set a backup password once, in **Settings → Controller → Backup → Automatic backups to your account** (ADR-048, docs/BACKUP.md).

- **Every night between 03:00 and 05:00** the controller sends a backup to your DirectorLink account.
  - It's locked so that only that password opens it. DirectorLink's servers can't open it, and neither can the controller.
  - The last 7 days are kept, one a day, up to 5 MB per home.
- **The buttons next to it:** Back up now, Change backup password, and Turn off.
- **To restore,** pick a date and type the password. The backup goes through the same check and Replace everything as a backup file.
- **If the backup password is lost, those backups can't be opened.** Keep it somewhere safe.
- It needs Remote Access on and the home linked to an account. Every admin of the home sees its backups.
- **The Sonos room choices are now in backups** (files and automatic) and come back with a restore. A backup made before 1.6.0 restores as before and leaves them as they are.

## Change a scene action

Each action in a scene now has an **Edit** button, or you can tap its text. The action opens filled in: where, which devices, and what it does.
- Tick or untick devices, switch between all and chosen ones, or change the setting or the room. Then **Save action** puts it back in its place.
- Devices removed in Composer are shown, so you can take them out.

This works with DirectorLink 1.5.0 on the controller too.

## A steadier remote connection

- **Faster checks:** DirectorLink checks its connection to the relay every 10 seconds instead of 25, so a connection that dies without a word is replaced within seconds.
- **No requests into a dead connection:** the relay waits a few seconds for the controller to reconnect, then sends them.
- **Free:** Cloudflare's runtime answers the checks without waking anything, so they cost nothing.
- Found from Cloudflare's logs: on some home networks a connection is cut silently, and Cloudflare never sees it end (ADR-045).

## Installing

- Update DirectorLink in Composer as usual: no reboot, and keys, scenes, schedules and Sonos rooms stay. The app updates by itself.
- History starts with the update. Alerts and automatic backups each need an admin to switch them on in the app.
- **Going back to 1.5.0:**
  - Everything 1.5.0 knows stays.
  - History, alerts and automatic backups stop until 1.6.0 is back; the backup password stays set.
  - 1.5.0 refuses a backup made on 1.6.0.

## For developers

- **New API routes:**
  - `GET /v1/activity` (admins; `kind`, `before`, `limit`);
  - `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE /v1/backup/automatic`, where PUT and DELETE are sealed only;
  - `POST /v1/backup/automatic/run`.
- **Other API changes:**
  - `GET /v1/system` has `features.automatic_backup`.
  - Backups have an optional `sonos_rooms` section.
  - Every change is additive; `api_version` stays `"1"`.
- **Relay protocol:**
  - The hello carries `ping_s`, and the `keys` message carries `admins`, the admin key ids.
  - New messages: `{"type":"alert","kind":"schedule_failed","at"}`, and `backup_chunk` with its `backup_result`.
- **Cloud:**
  - D1 migrations `0006_push_subscriptions` and `0007_cloud_backups`.
  - Web Push (VAPID, RFC 8291/8292): the `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` secret and the `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY` var.
  - `GET`, `POST` and `DELETE /v1/homes/{id}/alerts`.
  - `GET /v1/homes/{id}/backups[/{id}]` and `DELETE /v1/homes/{id}/backups`.
  - Stale sockets are logged as `driver_stale`.
- **CI:** validation runs in parallel jobs, about 4 minutes instead of 13. `driver/tests/run.lua --shard K/N` splits the driver suites.

## Known issues

- **Not yet seen on a real controller:**
  - history, alerts and automatic backups;
  - the backup's sealing time on a CORE-1 (estimated well under a second; the log's `seal_ms` gives the real figure).
- **History:** changes made in Composer while DirectorLink wasn't running aren't recorded.
- **Alerts:** a schedule that fails while the controller can't reach DirectorLink's servers sends no alert. If that lasts 10 minutes, the offline alert comes instead; a shorter outage alerts about neither. History still shows the failed schedule.
- **Remote connection:** a request already on its way when the connection dies silently can still fail. The app's own refresh asks again 2 s later.
