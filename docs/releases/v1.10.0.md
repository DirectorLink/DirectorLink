# DirectorLink v1.10.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

DirectorLink 1.10: Spanish and Italian, commands that do more (and in four languages), a text size, alerts on a page of their own, heaters left alone, Control4's relay gates and doors, scenes that turn each AC on as it was, weather schedules that work without the internet, no tap lost when the connection blinks, and one agreement for every DirectorLink camera driver, ready for UniFi Protect and the new DoorBird driver.

DirectorLink is an independent project, not affiliated with Control4 or Snap One.

## Spanish and Italian

The app now speaks **Spanish** and **Italian**, every screen and every alert (ADR-067). Pick it in **Settings → Appearance and language**. Dates, times and numbers follow the language. Commands understand them too (below).

## Appearance and language

Language, theme and colors have a page of their own: **Settings → Appearance and language** (מראה ושפה). Language, theme and colors follow you to all your devices.

**Text size** is new there: Small, Default, Large or Larger. It applies to the whole app, right from the start, and stays on this device (a phone and a big screen usually want different sizes). Text fields never get smaller than iPhones need to keep the page from zooming.

## Alerts have their own page

Alerts on this device moved from Settings → Controller to a page of their own: **Settings → Alerts** (התראות), next to Appearance and language. Its row tells you at a glance how alerts are on this device: on (and how many kinds), off, or what they still need, such as signing in or, on iPhone, adding the app to the Home Screen. The switch and the kinds are as before. Signed out, the page offers to sign in and brings you back to it.

## Commands that do more

- **Two or three things at once:** "kitchen lights off and close the blinds", "כבו את האורות בסלון ומזגן על 23". A room said once counts for the next part. It's all or nothing: if one part isn't clear, nothing runs, and it says which part (ADR-066).
- **Changes by a step:** "brighter", "a bit dimmer", "2 degrees warmer", "louder", "תגביר את האור", "תעלה את המזגן". An amount needs its unit ("by 20%", "2 degrees").
- **In Spanish and Italian**, as fully as English and Hebrew: "apaga las luces de la cocina y cierra las persianas", "spegni le luci della cucina e metti il condizionatore a 23", "sube la luz del salón" (ADR-068). English and Hebrew work in every app language; Spanish and Italian in their own.
- **Still never a guess.** A question (also "האור דלוק" without "?"), a time ("at 7", "a las siete"), a feeling ("I'm cold"), or "don't" never acts. Doors and Turn off all keep their second tap.

## Heaters left as they are

Lights named for heating (a boiler, floor heating, a towel warmer: "דוד הורים", "חימום רצפה", "termo", "scaldabagno") are no longer switched by "the lights" in a command, by Home's **Turn off all**, or by a room's **All off**. Each says which heaters it left. To switch a heater, use its own switch or say its name. Scenes and schedules are unchanged.

## Gates and doors of Control4's relay controllers

A gate or door that Control4 opens through a **Relay Door, Gate or Garage Door Controller** (a DoorBird's relay, a contact/relay module) is now a door or gate in DirectorLink too, with everything a door has: Open with two taps, History, the door-opened alert, ask-to-open links, scenes, favorites and commands by its name (ADR-069). DirectorLink opens it with the controller's own Open, exactly as Control4's programming does, and never closes or stops it. With a Closed or Opened contact bound, the app shows whether it is open ("Gate · Closed"). An opening from the Control4 app or a keypad is in History and the alert. A gate whose relay is a KNX relay DirectorLink already shows stays one gate, with its name and everything that refers to it.

## Scenes turn each AC on as it was

An AC action in a scene can now be **On, as it was**: each AC that is off comes back on in the mode it was last in, with its own temperature and fan, however it was last set (the Control4 app, a keypad, Composer programming or DirectorLink) (ADR-070). An AC already on is left alone. If DirectorLink hasn't seen an AC on since the update, it stays off and the scene and History say so: turn it on once and from then on it comes back. The temperature and fan of an AC action can also be **Keep**. "Turn on the AC in the living room" now turns it on in its last mode instead of asking which mode.

## Weather schedules work without the internet

The weather is now Open-Meteo's hourly forecast. The controller reads a 5-day forecast every 6 hours (4 requests a day instead of 96) and keeps it, so weather schedules and "only if" work the same for 5 days without the internet, Shabbat and holidays included (ADR-071). The app says "Forecast for 14:20, updated today 08:00", and Composer's Schedule Status says when the forecast was read. A forecast can be off by a degree or two, and the timing of rain by more.

## Weather schedules with hours start fresh each day

A weather schedule with hours is ready again each day when its hours begin. "When it's hotter than 23° outside, 08:00–23:00" now runs every day from 08:00 once it's that hot, even after a warm night that never cooled 2° below (before, it waited for the cooling). On Shabbat and holidays that means: the AC starts when Shabbat begins if it's hot, an evening scene turns it off, and the next morning it starts again from 08:00 once it's hot.

## Cameras: one agreement for every DirectorLink camera driver

- A camera driver of DirectorLink Drivers now says what it is with a few variables, and DirectorLink takes its alerts, its pictures and, for a doorbell, its rings, with no new code here (ADR-065). This is ready for the coming **DirectorLink · UniFi Protect** driver and the new **DirectorLink · DoorBird** driver. For driver authors: [docs/CAMERA_DRIVERS.md](https://github.directorlink.io/blob/main/docs/CAMERA_DRIVERS.md).
- **A doorbell camera** is listed with the doorbells: Home's banner with its live picture, the ring alert to everyone who sees it, and its last rings in its room.
- **Camera alerts** can also say Animal, Package and License plate.
- The Hikvision drivers keep working (1.0.x by file name, 1.1 by the agreement).

## A press is never lost when the connection blinks

Away from home, the app reaches the house through DirectorLink's servers. Some internet providers move a home's route to those servers every so often, which cuts the connection for a moment without a word; at the owner's home that happened every 20 to 60 minutes, day and night. DirectorLink was back within a second or two, but a tap in the seconds before it noticed, such as **Turn off all AC**, was lost.

Now a tap in that moment is carried out, a few seconds late, and only once: the servers keep it and send it again as soon as the home is back, and the controller remembers what it already did, so a door or a gate never opens twice. DirectorLink also checks its connection every 5 seconds instead of 10, so it notices a cut sooner.

## Installing

- **Deploy the cloud first** (it keeps working with DirectorLink 1.9.0 exactly as before), **then update DirectorLink in Composer** as usual. The app updates by itself. No D1 migration.
- **Going back to 1.9.0:** nothing is lost. Spanish and Italian stay in the profile (a 1.9.0 app shows them, but understands commands only in English and Hebrew); doorbell cameras are plain cameras again, and only the Hikvision camera driver's alerts arrive.

## For developers

- **Camera agreement v1** (docs/CAMERA_DRIVERS.md): `DIRECTORLINK_CAMERA`, `DIRECTORLINK_CAMERA_KIND`, `DIRECTORLINK_CAMERA_EVENTS` (recommended), `Alert`/`LAST_ALERT`, `Ring`/`LAST_RING`.
- **Doors:** every relay in `GET /v1/relays` has `kind` (`door`, `gate`, `garage_door` or `relay`) and `door_state` (`open`, `closed`, `partly_open` or null); `PATCH` on a relay controller's door answers 409 `NOT_SUPPORTED`.
- **Climate:** a scene's climate step may be `{"mode": "on"}` (alone); `GET /v1/thermostats` gives each `last_mode`; `features.climate_last_mode` in `GET /v1/system`; History's `counts.no_last_mode`.
- **Weather:** `GET /v1/weather` gains `source` (`forecast`), `forecast_for` and `forecast_until`; `wind_gusts` is always null.
- **API:** doorbell cameras in `/v1/doorbells` (`camera` is itself, `can_open` false; `POST …/open` answers 409 `NOT_SUPPORTED`) and in `/v1/devices?type=doorbell`. Every change is additive; `api_version` stays `"1"`.
- **App languages:** `app/i18n/es.js` and `it.js`; `scripts/check_app.py` checks every language has exactly the English keys and placeholders.
- **Relay (ADR-072, docs/RELAY.md):** the driver's `hello` lists `resend` and an `instance` (a random id per start) and says `ping_s` 5. A request already sent when the connection ends goes again, the same frame and id with `resent`, after the next `hello` from the same instance, at most twice and within 10 s; the driver runs each id once and answers a repeat from memory (`ANSWER_NOT_KEPT` when it no longer has the answer: `502 HOME_DISCONNECTED`, as before). Workers Observability logs `request_resent`. A Worker change; no D1 migration.

## Known issues

- **Not yet seen on a real controller:** a camera driver that sets the agreement (UniFi, the new DoorBird driver): whether its events are found (DirectorLink's log says how), and its rings.
- **Commands** don't do more than three things at once, steps for blinds and fans, or "warmer" without naming the AC.
- **Relay controllers, not yet seen on a real controller:** what Director gives for their relay connections and contacts, whether it gives their Relay Configuration (if not, they are taken as Pulse, the default), and their events reaching DirectorLink. TESTING 1100f checks them.
- **On, as it was** leaves an AC off until DirectorLink has seen it on once after the update.
