# DirectorLink v1.5.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

DirectorLink 1.5: Sonos in the app, a steadier remote connection, and a shorter Settings.

## Sonos

DirectorLink talks to the home's Sonos speakers itself, on the home network, with no Sonos account and no cloud (ADR-044, docs/SONOS.md).

- **Off until an installer turns it on.** In Composer, set DirectorLink's **Sonos** property to `On`.
  - **Sonos Players** then lists the speakers found.
  - If it says *None found* (speakers on another network), set **Sonos Address** to one speaker's IP address.
  - No restart is needed. Turning Sonos off stops everything at once.
- **Each room's screen** has a Music card for each Sonos room there: the album art, what's playing, and the other rooms of its group.
  - **Members and admins** play and pause, skip, set the volume and mute, and start a Sonos favorite.
  - **Viewers** see what plays.
- **Home** lists what's playing, with a pause button for each group.
- **Rooms:**
  - A Sonos room shows in the Control4 room with the same name, or with that room's name in another language set in the app.
  - Admins pick the room for the others in **Settings → Rooms → Sonos rooms**.
- **Groups** stay as Sonos has them. Play, pause and skip act on the whole group; volume and mute are each room's own.
- **Favorites** are your Sonos favorites. Some, such as Sonos Radio's shortcuts, only the Sonos app can start: they're greyed out.
- **Scenes** have a Music action: pause or stop the music in a room, or in the whole home.

## Remote access stays connected

ADR-045, docs/RELAY.md.

- **Fewer drops.** Director's own connection check, the likeliest cause of the drops every 10 to 40 minutes, is off. DirectorLink's ping every 25 s checks the connection instead.
- **Back in a second after a drop.** When a connection that was up for a minute or more drops (the home's internet, or a Cloudflare restart), the controller connects again after 1 second instead of 5. DirectorLink's servers hold a request for up to 8 seconds meanwhile, so the app away from home keeps working.
- **Every drop is explained.** Every drop and every reconnect attempt is in the relay log with its reason. Remote Status shows the last drop after reconnecting.
- **Two controllers with the same backup** that share one remote identity now take turns every 30 seconds instead of every few seconds.

## When another driver takes DirectorLink's port

Some drivers pick a random port each time the controller starts. On a real controller a camera driver once took 41999, DirectorLink's port. Director then refused it to DirectorLink without saying so. The app couldn't reach the controller at home, though remote access still worked.

- DirectorLink now notices within 15 seconds. Composer's **API Status** says *Port 41999 taken by another driver - retrying every minute*, and the log has an error.
- It asks for the port again every minute, so it has the port back within a minute of the other driver letting go.
- **The quick fix:** restart the controller. The other driver then picks another port.

## Settings

- **A short list.** Settings keeps Appearance and Language, then one row per page. Each row says in a line how things are.
  - The rows: Controller, Rooms, Shabbat and holidays, People and devices, Account, App and About.
  - **Back** returns to the list.
- **Controller** has the connection, Updates, Backup, Pair again and Forget access key. Its row has a badge when a newer DirectorLink is out.
- **Rooms** has the rooms you see, and for admins the order, the names and the Sonos rooms.
- **Check now** (admins), in Settings → Controller, asks at once whether a newer DirectorLink is out, instead of waiting for the next check (about twice a day).
  - It shows *Checking…*, then the answer.
  - It asks at most once a minute. Pressed sooner, it says *You can check again in a minute* and does nothing. The regular checks then count from it.
  - As before, only admin devices ask, and they ask GitHub directly, not through DirectorLink's servers.
- **App version:** Settings → Controller shows the app's own version next to DirectorLink's.

## Installing

- **Updates:**
  - Update DirectorLink in Composer as usual: no reboot, and keys, scenes and schedules stay.
  - The app updates by itself; reload an app that was open.
  - Sonos stays off until an installer turns it on.
- **Composer Pro is needed.** The README's installation steps now say so. Download it from [composer.directorlink.io](https://composer.directorlink.io), with an active Control4 account that has Composer Pro access.
- **Going back to 1.4.0:**
  - Everything else reads the same.
  - The Sonos room choices stay on the controller, unused until 1.5.0 is back.
  - 1.4.0 leaves out scenes' Music actions, and its log says so. Once any scene is saved, added or deleted there, or a backup restored, every scene's Music actions are gone for good; a scene with only Music actions is left empty.
  - 1.4.0 refuses a backup made on 1.5.0: the app says it *was made by a newer DirectorLink than this controller has*. Keep a backup made on 1.4.0 if you may go back.

## For developers

- **Sonos API:**
  - `GET /v1/music` (`?room_id=`);
  - `GET`/`PATCH /v1/music/{id}`;
  - `POST /v1/music/{id}/play|pause|next|previous`;
  - `GET /v1/music/{id}/favorites` and `POST …/favorites/{fid}/play`;
  - `GET /v1/music/{id}/art`;
  - `PUT /v1/music/{id}/room` (admins).
- **Other API changes:**
  - `GET /v1/system` has `features.sonos`.
  - Scenes take a `music` step (`pause` or `stop`, a room or the home).
  - Every change is additive; `api_version` stays `"1"`.
- **Driver:**
  - The relay connection opens with `MONITOR_CONNECTION = false`, and a global `OnPoll` answers Director's poll with a ping.
  - Relay log lines carry `reason`, `attempt`, `retry_s`, `up_s`, `heard_s` and `ping_s`.
- **Cloud:**
  - A request that finds no driver just after a disconnect, or after a deploy, waits up to 8 s for its hello (`RECONNECT_WAIT_MS`).
  - A request on a connection that a reconnect replaced fails at once (502).
  - `driver_disconnected` and `driver_connected` log `up_s`, `ping_s` and `down_ms`.
- **App:**
  - `app/js/version.js` holds the app's version; `scripts/check_app.py` keeps it equal to `VERSION`.
  - Settings pages live at `#/settings/<page>`.

## Known issues

- **Sonos:**
  - Read against the owner's real speakers.
  - Next, previous and the volume weren't tried on them; they were tested against fake speakers.
  - The search for speakers from a driver hasn't been seen on a real controller yet. **Sonos Address** is the way when it finds nothing.
  - Older S1 speakers haven't been tried.
  - The Sonos room choices aren't in backups yet.
- **The remote connection:**
  - That the drops stop on a real controller is still to be seen.
  - A request already on its way when the connection drops still fails. The app's own refresh asks again 2 s later; a command someone tapped shows an error, to be tapped again.
- **The port check** was tested against a fake Director. Director's refusal itself was seen on a real controller.
