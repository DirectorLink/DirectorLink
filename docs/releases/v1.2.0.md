# DirectorLink v1.2.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

DirectorLink 1.2: Shabbat and holiday times, worked out on the controller (off until an installer turns them on), rooms put in order by dragging them, fans, and the alarm's status, read-only (fans and the alarm thanks to @bkwagner, #18 and #15). Two rare cases left over from 1.1.1 are fixed too.

## Shabbat and holidays (off by default)

A new Composer property, **Jewish Calendar**, is **Off** by default, with a read-only **Calendar Status** under it. While it is Off, DirectorLink works out nothing and the app shows none of this. With it **On** (no restart), DirectorLink works out Shabbat and holiday times on the controller from the project's location; nothing goes to the network.

- **Schedules at candle lighting or havdalah**, up to six hours before or after (for example *30 min before candle lighting*). Shabbat and holidays that follow each other (Rosh Hashana on Thursday and Friday, then Shabbat) count as one: such a schedule runs once when they begin, or once when they end.
- **Any time, sun or weather schedule can skip Shabbat and holidays, or run only then**, counted from candle lighting to havdalah (not by the calendar day). A weather rule held back on Shabbat runs after havdalah if the weather still passes.
- **Schedules** shows the next candle lighting and havdalah (with the holidays' names), or the havdalah of the Shabbat or holiday now. **Home** shows today's Hebrew date (the next day's from sunset, within seconds of it), its holidays and the week's parasha, in Hebrew letters in Hebrew (כ״ד בתשרי תשפ״ז · פרשת בראשית).
- **Admins choose** Israel or abroad (automatic by default, from the location) and the minutes: candle lighting 20 before sunset and havdalah 42 after, by default (Settings → Shabbat and holidays).
- **After a controller restart on Shabbat**, Shabbat schedules and "only on Shabbat" schedules that were missed in the last six hours run once, marked *late after a restart*: nobody can put a missed "lights on" right by hand on Shabbat. Nothing runs twice, and what was due while the schedules were paused or the property Off is never caught up, not even by a restart.
- While the property is Off, or the project has no location, Shabbat schedules and "only on Shabbat" schedules are kept but do not run, and "not on Shabbat" schedules run every day; the app and Composer's Schedule Status say so.
- Composer's Calendar Status, Schedule Status, Last Automation and **Print Schedules and Scenes** describe all this.
- Tested against Hebcal's published data: the dates, holidays and weekly readings match for every year from 5760 to 5820, in Israel and abroad, and the candle-lighting and havdalah times for 2024–2035 match to the minute in Tel Aviv, New York, Buenos Aires and Reykjavik.

## Rooms in order by dragging them

- In **Settings → Rooms**, admins now drag a room by its handle (⠿) to set the home's order; on a phone, touch and hold the handle first. The list scrolls by itself near its ends, and each move is saved in one request (before, every tap on an arrow was one). The keyboard (Space, the arrow keys, Space) and the up and down arrows work too.

## Fans (thanks to @bkwagner, #18)

- DirectorLink now controls fans on the Control4 fan proxy (`fan.c4i`): on, off and four speeds (Low, Medium, Medium High, High).
- In the app, a fan appears in its room with a switch and a speed control, as a favorite on Home, on the room cards, and in the scene editor (Off, On or a speed). **Copy the house** includes fans, and **All off** in a room turns its fans off too. Viewers see fans without controls. Composer's Inventory counts fans.

## The alarm's status, read-only (thanks to @bkwagner, #15)

- A new Composer property, **Alarm Status**, after Door Control and Relay Hold, is **Off** by default. While it is Off, DirectorLink does not watch the alarm, the app shows nothing, and alarm partitions stay unsupported devices, as before.
- Set it to **On** (no restart), and members and admins see, on Home and in Settings → Controller, each partition of the alarm: *Armed away* or *Armed home*, *Disarmed, ready* or *not ready*, an *Entry delay* or *Exit delay* with its seconds counting down, *Alarm: fire* (or burglary…), open zones and trouble. Viewers never see it. Composer's Inventory adds the number of partitions.
- **Read-only**: DirectorLink never arms or disarms, not from the app, the API, scenes, schedules or Composer. Arming takes the user's alarm code, which needs a design of its own (ADR-038).
- Whether a home is armed never crosses a network in the clear: the alarm is only in sealed answers (the app's way, at home and through the account), padded so that their size does not tell it either. Very long texts from the panel are shortened.

## Fixed

- **Forget key could end on the wrong screen.** **Forget access key** or **Pair again** in Settings → Controller, pressed while the app was refreshing, connecting or checking that a light had switched, let what was already on its way carry on. The app went on asking the controller for a moment, with the key being revoked or with none, and could end on *Can't reach your controller*, or even show the home as connected, instead of the pairing screen. It now stops all of that first, sends nothing more, and ends on the pairing screen with *The access key was removed from this device.* The key was never at risk: it was already revoked, and sealed requests never carry it.
- **A shade could say *Opening…* for good after a controller restart.** After a restart, Control4 can leave a shade marked as not stopped while its position and its last target are both known and a little apart (49% and 50%, say). On shades whose Control4 driver reports opening and closing (every KNX shade), a shade now counts as moving only while those say so, or in the moment a move starts. After Stop, a shade also shows as stopped a moment sooner. This has not been seen on the test system.
- **Sunrise and sunset** are now worked out with NOAA's solar calculator, the way Hebcal works out candle lighting. Existing sunrise and sunset schedules may move by up to a minute (two in the far north).

## For developers

- Calendar: `GET /v1/calendar` (with `today.changes_at`, when the Hebrew date changes next) and `PATCH /v1/calendar/settings` (with `version`; `409 VERSION_CONFLICT`), `features.jewish_calendar` in `GET /v1/system`, the schedule trigger `{"type": "shabbat", "event": "candle_lighting" | "havdalah", "offset": -360..360}`, `during_shabbat` (`run`, `skip`, `only`) and `calendar_status` on schedules, `last_run.skipped_by: "shabbat"` and `last_run.note: "late"`, and `409 JEWISH_CALENDAR_OFF` for setting any of them while the property is Off.
- Fans: `GET /v1/fans`, `GET` and `PATCH /v1/fans/{id}` with `{"on": …}` or `{"speed": 1-4}`, scene steps of type `fans`, `inventory.fans` in `/v1/system`, and the `fan` type in `/v1/devices`.
- Alarm: `GET /v1/alarm` for member keys and above, only in sealed requests (scripts and the API console get `403 SEALED_REQUEST_REQUIRED`), its JSON followed by spaces to a size that depends only on the partitions; the panel's words cut to 32 bytes, `trouble` to 100; `features.alarm_status` in `/v1/system`.
- `GET /v1/blinds` answers `"moving": false` for a shade whose proxy has Opening and Closing, both 0, even while Stopped is 0 and Level and Target Level differ, except in the first seconds after a new Target Level (a move starting). Proxies without Opening and Closing are unchanged.
- Every change is additive; `api_version` stays `"1"`.
- The calendar engine (`src/core/hebrew_date.lua`, `holidays.lua`, `parasha.lua`, `holy_times.lua`, `sun.lua`) is pure Lua and never uses the network (`check_package.py` enforces it); it is tested against Hebcal.com data (CC BY 4.0) in `tests/vectors/calendar`, which `node scripts/make_calendar_vectors.mjs` regenerates (docs/CALENDAR.md). `lua5.1 driver/tests/run.lua <suites>` runs single suites; `dev_server.py --jewish-calendar` previews the calendar.

## Installing

Update DirectorLink in Composer as usual (it loads without a reboot; keys, scenes and schedules stay). The app updates by itself; reload an app that was open during the update. After the update DirectorLink has three new properties, all off: **Jewish Calendar** (`Off`) with **Calendar Status**, and **Alarm Status** (`Off`). Turn them on only where they are wanted.

Going back to 1.1.x: schedules with a Shabbat trigger are lost at the next save of any schedule, and "not on Shabbat" and "only on Shabbat and holidays" are dropped: "not on Shabbat" schedules then run on Shabbat too, and "only on Shabbat and holidays" ones on every day they are set for (every morning, for one at 08:00 every day). Switch the "only on Shabbat and holidays" schedules off before going back, and set both again after returning to 1.2. The calendar's settings survive. Fan scene steps and fans in rooms are not known to 1.1.x.

## Known issues

- Check the times against your community's calendar the first time. Above about 66° latitude there are no times around midsummer and midwinter, and a Shabbat or holiday without one of its times runs neither its candle-lighting nor its havdalah schedules. Automatic Israel also covers parts of Jordan, Lebanon and Sinai: set Abroad there.
- A 1.1.x app still open during the update shows a Shabbat schedule as a rain rule and would save it as a heat rule, and shows a fan step in a scene as *Open (short press)*; saving that scene in it, or **Try it now**, drops the fan steps that name fans. Reload the app.
- Every fan is taken to have four speeds (Low, Medium, Medium High, High). On a fan with three, the top speed shows as *Medium High* and *High* asks for a speed it does not have; on a fan with five or more, the speeds above four show only as *On*.
- Fans and the alarm were read on the contributor's Director; no DirectorLink command has run on a real fan, and no DirectorLink build against a real alarm, yet (the test system has neither).
