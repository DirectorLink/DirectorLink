# DirectorLink v0.14.0

**Direct to Director. End-to-end integration. Open source.**

Schedules: your scenes run by themselves — on time, with the sun, or when the weather turns.

## Schedules

- A new **Schedules** page, next to Scenes. Each schedule runs a scene, written as a sentence: *Sun–Thu at 06:45 · Runs Good morning · Only if it isn’t raining · Next: tomorrow 06:45*.
- **At a time**, on the days you pick (with shortcuts for every day, Sun–Thu and Fri–Sat).
- **At sunrise or sunset**, or up to an hour before or after — worked out on the controller for your home's location.
- **When the weather turns:**
  - **Heat:** when it's hotter than, say, 30° outside ("Cool the house"); it runs again only after it has cooled a little.
  - **Wind:** when the wind is stronger than, say, 40 km/h ("Close all blinds").
  - **Rain:** when it starts to rain.
  - Limit them to certain hours and days, and to once a day.
- **Only if:** a time or sunset schedule can run only if it isn't raining, it's hot, the wind is calm, or rain is forecast today — and you choose what happens when there is no weather data.
- Switch any schedule off and on from the list.
- The controller runs schedules by itself, even when no phone is open, and a restart never runs one twice.
- Schedules never open doors or gates: a scene's door steps are skipped when a schedule runs it.

## The weather

- Weather from **Open-Meteo**: the temperature, wind and rain now, and today's forecast, shown on the Schedules page with sunrise and sunset.
- Your controller asks Open-Meteo directly, every 15 minutes while a schedule needs it; nothing goes through DirectorLink's servers. The location is the one set in your Control4 project (Composer → project properties), rounded to about a kilometre. The privacy page says so.

## Also

- A scene that schedules run can't be deleted until they are changed.

## Installing

Update DirectorLink in Composer (it loads without a reboot; keys, profiles, scenes and settings stay). The app updates by itself. For weather schedules, make sure the project has its latitude and longitude in Composer.

## Known issues

- Weather schedules need the controller to reach the internet; time and sunrise/sunset schedules do not.
- Only relay 1 of each KNX Contact/Relay device is controlled; KNX percentage dimming remains deferred (issue #11).
