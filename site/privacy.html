Privacy

Last updated 27 September 2026

DirectorLink keeps your home's data between your own devices and your controller. This page lists exactly what reaches DirectorLink's servers.

On your home network

The app talks to the DirectorLink driver on your controller directly, over your home network. Device names, states, commands and camera pictures do not pass through our servers. The app keeps its settings, its access key and the controller's address in your browser only.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. It is for using your home away from the home network and for inviting family. When you sign in, Google tells us your Google account ID, your email address and your name. We ask for nothing else, never see your Google password, and use these only to know which homes your account belongs to and to match invitations. We do not sell or share them, and there are no ads, tracking or analytics.

A device that has never signed in does not contact our account service at all.

What our servers store

  • Your account: your Google account ID, email address and name, and when you signed in.
  • Sessions: a random identifier, kept only as a hash, for 30 days or until you sign out.
  • With remote access and invitations (coming): which homes your account belongs to, and invitations waiting to be accepted (the invited email and the expiry date).

Our servers run on Cloudflare, which keeps short-lived technical logs (time, IP address, path and status) to keep the service running.

Remote access

Remote access is off unless you switch it on in Composer. Today it is a test: the controller keeps one outgoing connection to api.directorlink.io, encrypted with TLS, and only the DirectorLink team's test tool can send it read-only requests, which are passed through and not stored. When remote access opens to accounts, every request and answer will also be locked end to end, with keys that only your devices and your controller hold, so our servers can pass them along but cannot read them.

Cookies

api.directorlink.io sets one cookie that keeps you signed in, and one for ten minutes while you sign in. Neither can be read by the app's pages. The app and this website set no cookies.

Deleting your data

Signing out ends the session on that device. Settings → Account → Delete account in the app deletes your account and signs out all your devices.

Contact
