DirectorLink Drivers

DirectorLink · Android TV Notifications for Control4

A free, open-source Control4 driver that shows notifications as a pop-up with a picture on TVs that run Google TV or Android TV: a doorbell ring with who is at the door, a camera alert, a door or gate opened, the alarm. It talks straight to PiPup, a free app on the TV: no cloud, no account and no extra hardware.

Free. No subscription, no license key, no account with us.

Download the driver Release notes and checksums Source and full guide

Made by DirectorLink, the open-source management layer for Control4 homes. Works on its own: DirectorLink is not required, and Composer programming can show pop-ups too.

What you get

Feature On the TV Programming
A pop-up on the TV Over whatever is on, in the corner you choose or the center, for 15 seconds or as long as you set Show Notification, with a title, a message and a picture's address
The picture Who is at the door or the gate, in the pop-up: the picture DirectorLink sends with a ring, or any camera's snapshot Picture URL, for example a camera's snapshot address
DirectorLink's alerts Doorbell rings, camera alerts, doors and gates opened, the alarm and others, with DirectorLink 1.13 and newer –
Your choice for each TV Doorbell, Camera Alerts, Doors and Gates, Alarm and Other, each on or off (Doorbell and Alarm are on) –
Quiet Hours No pop-ups on this TV at night, or whenever you set Show Notification keeps to them too
Several TVs One copy of the driver for each TV, with its own kinds, Quiet Hours and look; DirectorLink's alerts show on every TV that is on, at the same moment Show Notification with Send To All TVs: one command reaches every TV
What happened – Notification Shown and Notification Failed events, the last notification's text

Just before each pop-up the driver asks PiPup whether the screen is on: off, or no answer within 3 seconds, and the pop-up is dropped. The driver never turns the TV on or changes its channel, input or volume. At most one pop-up every 5 seconds on each TV, a doorbell or the alarm first; a new one replaces the one still showing. The text shows as it was sent, Hebrew and other right-to-left languages included. The controller fetches the picture and sends it inside the pop-up, so the TV never has to reach the camera.

The app on the TV

The TV runs PiPup — TV Notifications by Alexander Savin, free on Google Play. It shows a pop-up when a device on the home network asks, with no cloud and no account, and starts by itself when the TV starts. Only this PiPup: other apps called PiPup take other requests and do not work with the driver.

The same brand often ships different systems in different countries: look in the TV's own Settings → About. Composer lists the driver as DirectorLink · Android TV Notifications; the file is DirectorLink-Android-TV.c4z.

What it needs

  • A Control4 controller on OS 3.3.0 or newer, and Composer Pro.
  • A TV or box with Google TV or Android TV, Android 7 or newer, with Google Play and PiPup. Tested on a Sony XR-77A95L (Google TV) with PiPup 0.3.4; reports from other TVs are welcome.
  • A fixed IP address for the TV in the router. The controller reaches PiPup on port 7979, or the port set in PiPup.
  • For DirectorLink's alerts, DirectorLink 1.13 or newer.

Setup in four steps

  1. 1

    PiPup on the TV

    On the TV, install PiPup — TV Notifications from Google Play. Open it once, select Grant display permission, and press its test button: a pop-up shows.

  2. 2

    Add the driver

    In Composer Pro, add DirectorLink · Android TV Notifications to the room of the TV, once for each TV.

  3. 3

    Pick the TV

    Pick the TV in TVs Found, where PiPup's TVs come first, marked PiPup answers, or type its IP address. Status says Ready.

  4. 4

    Test and choose

    Run Test Notification. Then choose what this TV shows, Quiet Hours, and the look: Position, Seconds On Screen and Pictures.

Leave PiPup's token and IP allowlist off, or enter the same token in PiPup Token and add the controller's address to the allowlist: Status says when PiPup wants either. Every setting and what each Status means are on the driver's Documentation tab in Composer and in the full guide.

With DirectorLink

DirectorLink 1.13 and newer sends its alerts to this driver as soon as both are in the project, with nothing to set up: the doorbell with the picture of who is at the door, camera alerts with the camera's picture, doors and gates opened, and, with Alarm Status on in DirectorLink, the alarm going off, as each TV's choices allow. They are in the home owner's language, in the same words as on the family's phones, and they come straight from the controller: no phone needs alerts switched on, and the internet can be down. The driver follows DirectorLink's notification agreement, the same as DirectorLink · LG TV Notifications.

Ideas for programming

  • The gate on every TV

    When the gate opens, Show Notification: Gate, Opened, the gate camera's snapshot address, Send To All TVs.

  • A camera with a login

    Write the login in the snapshot address, http://user:password@…: the controller sends it as a login and never logs it.

  • A fallback

    On Notification Failed, send the text as a push notification instead.

Privacy and support

  • The driver talks only to PiPup on the TV, to the picture's address it is given (the controller, a camera), and, for Find TVs, to the casting devices that answer the search on the home network. It sends nothing to DirectorLink, Google, the app's maker or anyone else, and collects no usage data. PiPup's token and a login in a picture's address are never written to the log.
  • Keep the exact file name: a browser's … (1).c4z copy installs as a separate driver instead of updating. An update keeps the settings and the programming.
  • Community-supported, best effort. Report a problem with the TV model and its Android or Google TV version, PiPup's version (the TV property), your Control4 OS version, the driver's Print Diagnostics and its log at Debug.
