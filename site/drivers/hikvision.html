DirectorLink Drivers

DirectorLink · Hikvision for Control4

Free, open-source Control4 drivers for Hikvision IP cameras and NVRs. Add one driver, type the camera login once, and every camera on the network is in Control4, named and ready. They work on your local network: no cloud, no extra hardware and no subscription.

Free. No subscription, no license key, no account with us.

Download the drivers Release notes and checksums Source and full guide

Made by DirectorLink, the open-source management layer for Control4 homes. Works on its own: DirectorLink is not required.

Control4 plays H.264 video only. The hub switches each camera's sub stream to H.264 when it adds it; the main stream, which the NVR records, is not touched.

Two drivers, install both

Driver in Composer File What it does
DirectorLink · Hikvision DirectorLink-Hikvision.c4z Added once (it shows as Hikvision Hub): finds every camera and NVR, adds them with one login, and gives the home one alerts tile.
DirectorLink · Hikvision Camera DirectorLink-Hikvision-Camera.c4z One per camera, added by the hub (or by hand): video, detections, alerts, controls and programming.

What it needs

  • A Control4 controller on OS 3.2 or newer (tested on 4.2.4), and Composer Pro.
  • Hikvision IP cameras and NVRs with ISAPI, which covers almost all current models. Tested with AcuSense and ColorVu G2 cameras and a K-series NVR.
  • The cameras on the controller's network. Cameras on other subnets or VLANs are listed by address in the hub.
  • One login that works on the cameras, and H.264 on at least one stream (the hub sets it).

Setup in three steps

  1. 1

    Add the drivers

    In Composer Pro: Driver → Add or Update Driver or Agent, once for each file. Search for DirectorLink and add DirectorLink · Hikvision to the room where new cameras should appear.

  2. 2

    Enter the login once

    In the Hikvision Hub's properties, set Username and Password. The hub searches the network and lists what it found.

  3. 3

    Add them

    Run Actions → Add New Cameras. The hub checks the login first, then adds, names and sets up each camera. Drag each one to its room.

For events, turn each detection on in the camera (or NVR) with Notify Surveillance Center ticked. Each step, NVRs, other subnets and what each message means are on the drivers' Documentation tab in Composer and in the full guide.

Ideas for programming

  • One notification for every camera

    On the hub's Camera Alert, send a notification with the snapshot from the camera that raised it.

  • Alerts follow the alarm

    Turn alerts on when the security system arms, and off when it disarms.

  • Lights for people at night

    On Person Detected, if it is dark, turn on the porch lights, even with alerts off.

Privacy and support

  • The drivers talk only to the Hikvision cameras and NVRs on your home network. They send nothing to DirectorLink and collect no usage data. Camera logins stay in your Control4 project, and reports and logs never show passwords.
  • Keep the exact file names: a browser's … (1).c4z copy installs as a separate driver instead of updating. Cameras keep their settings, rooms and programming when you update.
  • Community-supported, best effort. Report a problem with the camera or NVR model and firmware, your Control4 OS version and each driver's Driver Version.
